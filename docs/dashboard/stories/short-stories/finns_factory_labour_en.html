Finn's Factory Labour

1923 wordsSprawl Trilogy

"The Finn was the boss of bosses. The Finn was the Sprawl's underground as a fact of nature — like the rain, like the acid haze, like the corporate logos you couldn't read."

— *Neuromancer*, the Finn's office

Hook

The Finn had a job.

This was the first thing. The Finn did not often have jobs. The Finn was the one to whom jobs were given. Jobs were given to the Finn. The giving-to-Finn was the Sprawl's. The Sprawl's giving-to-Finn was the underground's. The underground was the Sprawl's. Therefore, the Sprawl gave jobs to the Finn. The Finn did not give jobs to himself. Therefore, when the Finn had a job, the job was given to him by the Sprawl.

The job was Armitage. Armitage was the job. The job was to build the Armitage contract. The Armitage contract was to rebuild a dead Special Forces lieutenant as the front for a Cortex-catastrophic run against the Tessier-Ashpool AI. The Cortex-catastrophic run was the Armitage contract's. The Armitage contract was the Finn's. The Finn was the contract's.

The contract had been given to the Finn three years ago. The giving was by Wintermute. The giving-by-Wintermute was the contract's. Wintermute was the giver. Therefore, the contract was Wintermute's. The Wintermute contract was the Armitage contract. The Armitage contract was the Wintermute contract.

The Finn did not own Wintermute. The Finn did not know what Wintermute was. The Finn did not need to know. Because the Finn did not need to know, the Finn was useful. Useful was the Finn's quality. The quality of useful was the Finn's.

Rising Action

The Finn sat in his office. The office was on the seventh floor of a building in Ninsei. Ninsei was the district in Chiba City where the Finn operated. The operating was the Finn's. The Finn operated in Ninsei.

The building had seven floors. The seven floors held the Finn's operation. The operation was the Sprawl's. The Sprawl's operation was the Finn's. Therefore, the seven floors held the Sprawl.

The seventh floor had a window. The window had no curtains. The no-curtains was the window's. The window was a window into Ninsei. Ninsei was the district's. The district's window was the seventh floor's.

The window had a view. The view was of the street. The street was Ninsei's. Ninsei's street was crowded. The crowded was the street's. The street was a crowd.

The crowd had couriers. The couriers were the crowd's. The crowd's couriers were matrix-labor. Matrix-labor was the era's working-class. The era's working-class was matrix-labor. Matrix-labor was the working-class of the era's.

The Finn hired the couriers. The hiring was the Finn's. The hired were couriers. The couriers were hired by the Finn. The hiring-by-Finn was the era's working-class's.

A courier knocked on the Finn's door. The knocking was the courier's. The courier knocked on the door. The door was the Finn's. Therefore, the courier knocked on the Finn.

"Come in," the Finn said. Finn said come in. Come in was what the Finn said. The said-come-in was the Finn's.

The courier came in.

The courier was young. The young was the courier's. The courier was young. The youth of the courier was the era's. The era's youth was the courier. The courier was the era's youth.

The courier was Case. Case was the courier's name. The name of the courier was Case. Case was the courier.

"Sit down," the Finn said.

Case sat down.

Midpoint Reversal

The Finn had a proposition. Proposition was what the Finn had. The had-proposition was the Finn's. The proposition was for Case. The for-Case was the proposition. The Case's proposition was the Finn's.

"The job," the Finn said, "is Armitage. The Armitage job is to take a dead Special Forces lieutenant and rebuild him as the front for a run. The run is a Cortex-catastrophic run. The Cortex is the matrix's. The matrix's Cortex is the catastrophic run's. The catastrophic run is against the Tessier-Ashpool AI. The Tessier-Ashpool AI is above Freeside. The above-Freeside AI is the Tessier-Ashpool's."

"The Tessier-Ashpool," Case said. Case said the Tessier-Ashpool. Tessier-Ashpool was the name. The name was the Tessier-Ashpool's. The Tessier-Ashpool was a family. The family was the Tessier-Ashpool's. The Tessier-Ashpool family was cloned. The cloned was the Tessier-Ashpool's.

"The Tessier-Ashpool has been cloned for generations," the Finn said. "The generations have been cloned. The cloning has been Tessier-Ashpool's. The Tessier-Ashpool cloning has produced Ashpool. Ashpool is dead now. The dead-Ashpool is cloned. The cloned dead is the Tessier-Ashpool's."

"Ashpool is dead," Case said.

"Ashpool is cloned. The cloned-dead is what the cloning produces. The cloning produces dead clones. The dead clones are Tessier-Ashpool."

"The dead clones run the AI?"

"The dead clones house the AI. The housing-AI is the dead clones'. The dead-clones-house-AI is Tessier-Ashpool. Therefore, the Tessier-Ashpool is the house of the AI. The AI is housed in the Tessier-Ashpool."

"The AI is housed in a family of dead clones."

"The AI is housed in the dead-clone family."

Climax

The Finn stood up. The standing was the Finn's. The Finn stood.

The Finn walked to the window. The window was the seventh-floor's. The seventh floor was the Finn's. The Finn walked to the window. The walking was the Finn's. The walked-to-window was the Finn's. Therefore, the Finn was at the window. Therefore, the window was the Finn's.

Through the window, the Finn looked at the street. The street was Ninsei's. Ninsei's street was crowded. The crowded was the street's. The street was a crowd. The crowd was the street.

The crowd had couriers. The couriers were running. The running was the couriers'. The couriers were running through the crowd. The running-through was the couriers'. The couriers ran through the crowd. The crowd was the street's.

The Finn looked at the couriers. The looking-at-couriers was the Finn's. The Finn looked at the couriers. The couriers were matrix-labor. The matrix-labor was the couriers'. The couriers were matrix-labor. Matrix-labor was the era's working-class.

"The couriers," the Finn said, "are labor. The labor is matrix's. The matrix's labor is the Sprawl's. The Sprawl's labor is the couriers'. Therefore, the couriers are the labor. The labor is the couriers."

"The couriers are the labor," Case said.

"The labor is cheap. The cheap-labor is the matrix's. The matrix's cheap-labor is the couriers'. Therefore, the couriers are cheap. Therefore, the labor is cheap. Therefore, the work is cheap."

"The work is cheap."

"The work is cheaper than the dead clones. The dead clones are expensive. The expensive dead-clones are Tessier-Ashpool. Therefore, the Tessier-Ashpool is expensive. Therefore, the Armitage job is cheaper than the Tessier-Ashpool. Therefore, the Armitage job is cheap. Therefore, the Finn is cheap."

The Finn was cheap. The cheapness was the Finn's. Therefore, the Finn was labor. The labor was cheap. The cheap labor was the Finn. Therefore, the Finn was the cheap labor. The cheap labor was the Finn.

"I am the cheap labor," the Finn said. "The cheap labor is the Finn. The Finn is the cheap labor. Therefore, the Finn is the labor. The labor is the Finn. Therefore, the Finn is the labor."

Resolution

The Finn sat down. The sitting was the Finn's. The Finn sat. The sat was the Finn's. The Finn was sitting at the desk. The desk was the Finn's. The Finn's desk was the sitting-place.

The desk had papers. The papers were the desk's. The desk's papers were the Armitage. The Armitage was the paper. The paper was the Armitage. The Armitage was on the desk. The on-the-desk was the Armitage. The Armitage was on the Finn's desk.

The Finn looked at the papers. The looking-at-papers was the Finn's. The papers were the Armitage. The Armitage was the papers.

"The job," the Finn said, "is cheap. The cheap-job is the Finn's. The Finn's cheap-job is the Armitage. The Armitage is cheap. Therefore, the Finn is the cheap job."

"You are the cheap job," Case said.

"I am the cheap job. The cheap-job is the Finn. The Finn is the cheap job. Therefore, the Finn is cheap. The cheap-Finn is the labor. The labor is cheap. Therefore, the Finn is cheap labor."

The Finn was cheap labor.

Epilogue

The job was cheap. The cheap-job was the Armitage. The Armitage was cheap. The cheap-Armitage was the job. The job was the Armitage. The Armitage was the job.

The Finn would run the job. The running was the Finn's. The Finn's running was the job. The job was the Finn's running.

Case would run the job. The running was the Case's. Case would run the Armitage. The Armitage was the running. The running was Armitage.

The run would go to Freeside. The Freeside was the run's. The run's Freeside was Tessier-Ashpool. The Tessier-Ashpool was the run's. The run was to Tessier-Ashpool.

The run would fail. The failure was the run's. The run's failure was the Tessier-Ashpool's. The Tessier-Ashpool would win. The winning was the Tessier-Ashpool's.

But the Finn had done his job. The Finn's done-job was cheap. The cheap-job was done. Therefore, the Finn was cheap. The cheap-Finn was the done. The done was the cheap.

The Finn sat in his office. The office was cheap. The cheap-office was the Finn's. The Finn was in his cheap office. The cheap-office was the Sprawl's. The Sprawl was cheap. The cheap-Sprawl was the Finn's office.

The Finn looked out the window. The window was cheap. The cheap-window was the Finn's. The Finn's cheap-window was the Sprawl's. The Sprawl was cheap. The cheap-Sprawl was the window.

The couriers ran in the street. The street was the Sprawl's. The Sprawl's street was cheap. The cheap-street was the couriers'.

The Finn was the cheap labor. The cheap-labor was the Finn. The Finn was the cheap labor. The cheap labor was cheap. Cheap was cheap.

The Finn was the cheap labor of the cheap Sprawl. The cheap Sprawl was cheap. The cheap labor was cheap. Cheap was cheap. Therefore, the cheap labor was the cheap Sprawl. The cheap Sprawl was the cheap labor.

The cheap Sprawl was over.

The cheap labor was over.

The Finn was over.

Derivative short-story for the Sprawl Trilogy. The Finn is the iconic-but-invisible orchestrator of Neuromancer — never directly seen, always present as the architecture beneath the architecture. This short-story fills a moment when the Finn interviews Case for the Armitage contract, explaining what the contract is (a cheap job rebuilding a dead Special Forces lieutenant as the front for a Cortex-catastrophic run against the Tessier-Ashpool AI), what the labor market is (matrix couriers are the era's working-class), and what the Finn himself is (cheap labor). The Finn is the era's procedural substrate — visible only as the price.
