Laney's Nodal Point

A Pattern-Perceiver Crosses the Bridge at the Edge of Sleep

274 wordsBridge Trilogy

Laney crossed the bridge on foot at three in the morning. The bridge was empty except for one courier. The courier did not look at him.

Laney walked. The walk had been the walk since Tokyo.

The data wanted to organize. The data had been wanting to organize for nineteen years. The organizing did not ask permission.

A nodal point appeared at the third cable. The cable was the cable that did not, in any record he had been given, move. The cable moved.

Laney stopped. He had not stopped since the party. The party had been a Loveless party.

The nodal point brightened. The point was, in his private count, the point at which the Bridge was about to do something the Bridge had not done before. The bridge had been a bridge since 1936.

Laney watched. He had not written anything down since the military. The military had been the military of a kind that did not, in any record he had been given, recognize what he saw.

A second point appeared at the seventh cable. The cable was the cable of a kind made in 1933 by a man who had since drowned.

Laney sat on the deck. The deck was cold.

He closed his eyes. The data kept coming. The data had not stopped since 1987.

A third point appeared at the eleventh cable. The cable was, in any record he had been given, the cable that had broken in 1989. The cable had been welded. The weld had held.

Laney slept for the first time in nine days.

He slept because the data let him.
