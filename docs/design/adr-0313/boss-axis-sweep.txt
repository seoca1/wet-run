ADR-0313 — the boss axis has no room (ADR-0312 follow-up)

WHY THIS EXISTS
  ADR-0312 measured 100% of g6 mission deaths at the boss and 0 in ordinary
  encounters. That looked like a shape defect with a cheap fix: move some
  difficulty off the boss and into the fights. This probe tests that.

INSTRUMENT
  per-cell (ADR-0310). Mission rows = 128 seeds (384 runs), `--grade 6`,
  optimal policy. Enc rows = the per-encounter balance harness (opening deck).

KNOB 1 — BOSS HP (BOSS_HP_BY_GRADE[5], now 165)
  enc -10 (unchanged), 64 seeds:

    boss HP   g6 mission   boss share
     165        32.3%       100%
     140        66.7%       100%     +34.4pp for 25 HP
     120        80.7%       100%     +48.4pp for 45 HP

  A cliff. There is no HP value that trims the wall by a few points.

KNOB 2 — ENCOUNTER DAMAGE (ENCOUNTER_DMG_BONUS_PCT_BY_GRADE[5], now -10)
  boss 165, 128 seeds:

    enc    g6 mission   enc g6   normal deaths   boss share
    -10      30.7%       32.3%        0             100%
     -5      27.9%       29.7%        1             100%
     -3      29.7%        4.7%        3              99%
      0      26.6%        2.0%        6              96%

  Raising the encounter damage is the only way to make ordinary fights lethal
  — but the per-encounter curve is a violent step function (29.7% at -5,
  4.7% at -3). And it costs mission completion.

KNOB 3 — THE COMBINATION THAT LANDS ON TARGET
  g5 and g6 are coupled by a test (`iceDmgBonusPctForGrade(6) <= (5)`), so g6
  cannot rise alone. With BOTH at 0 and boss 160:

    grade   mission   target   enc
      5      24.1%      25%    46.7%
      6      29.9%      30%     2.0%     normal deaths 7, boss share 97%

  Both late grades land on target and the deaths spread. But the opening-deck
  reference curve moves hard (g5 83.9 -> 46.7, g6 32.3 -> 2.0), and the
  player-visible change is that 7 of 384 g6 runs (1.8%) now die in a fight
  instead of at the boss.

DECISION
  KEEP. The blast radius (two grades, two knobs) is out of proportion to the
  effect (1.8% of runs change where they die). g6 is already on target
  (30.7% at 128 seeds against 30). Every lever is a cliff, so a future tweak
  cannot be small.

  ADR-0290's decision ("the late grades hit no harder than g4") is satisfied
  either way, and the option above is legal — it is simply not worth it.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE THREAD
  This is the second consecutive lever measured and kept (ADR-0312 was the
  first). The g6/curve thread has no remaining knob that buys a player-visible
  change at a proportional cost. The next work belongs to a different axis
  (run shape, economy, content), not to this one.

REPRODUCE
  cd web
  # patch ENCOUNTER_DMG_BONUS_PCT_BY_GRADE[4..5] and BOSS_HP_BY_GRADE[5], then:
  npm run survival -- --seeds 128 --grade 6 --cards best --loot value --deaths
  npm run survival -- --seeds 128 --grade 5 --cards best --loot value
  npm run balance
