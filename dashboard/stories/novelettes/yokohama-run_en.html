Yokohama Run

"Things have always looked like things. The footage just shows us that."

Introduction

Yokohama lies thirty kilometers south of Tokyo, where the bay meets the city. Slower than Tokyo, warmer than Tokyo, and closer to the waterfront. Therefore, Yokohama is the city of escape. If Tokyo is the city of refusal, then Yokohama is the city of avoidance. Consequently, escape and avoidance are not the same. Refusal is facing; avoidance is turning.

August 2002, an hour south of Shibuya, Tokyo. Cayce Pollard's last lead on the Footage. The Footage may have begun in Yokohama. Its avatar surfaced in Tokyo, but the true source could be elsewhere.

cayce-pollard|Cayce rides a black fixie through the August humidity. The red pedal catches the sodium-light. She has not slept in twenty-six hours. Her watch, a Korean clone of an old-school Casio G-Shock sanded free of logos, reads 4:17 a.m. The Manseibashi overpass throws a long shadow across the bike lane, and therefore the air under it is three degrees cooler. She does not slow. Because slowing is refusal. Refusal is culture. Culture is the city.

Rising Action

"Cayce." hollis-henry|Hollis Henry spoke from a payphone in Azabu. Node magazine reporter. Voice flattened by the satellite delay. "There is something in Yokohama. The first distribution point for the footage. 1997. Someone saw the original footage there."

"Why Yokohama?" Cayce asked.

"Yokohama is a port," Hollis said. "Tokyo is the interior. Yokohama is the outside. The footage came from the outside. Therefore, Yokohama is logical."

Cayce rode the fixie. Fog swallowed Yokohama. The fog tasted of salt and diesel, of burnt sugar from the chinmi stalls on Isezakichō. Fog was culture. Culture was refusal. Refusal was culture. Therefore, fog was culture.

The port-side wind brought a faint chemical smell — paint stripper, maybe, or the polymer that the new water-buses used for their hulls. Cayce's skin prickled. However, her allergy did not fire. Therefore, whatever lived in Yokohama was not a brand. It was a pattern. Patterns preceded brands. Patterns outlived brands. Because of this, patterns were more dangerous than brands. Therefore, Cayce was afraid.

"Cayce." A local fixer spoke. A woman in a coolhunter|coolhunter's borrowed jacket, brandless, oversized at the shoulders. "Yokohama is waterfront. The footage came from the sea. The sea is memory. Memory is culture."

"The footage is memory?" Cayce asked.

"The footage is culture's memory," the local fixer said. "Japan is the culture of memory. Memory does not rust. What does not rust is culture. Culture is me. I too am memory. Memory is culture."

Cayce thought of hubertus-bigend|Bigend in his soho|SoHo office, three weeks earlier, pale blue eyes like a fish's, his 6'6" frame folded into a chair that had not been built for him. Therefore, he had built the chair. Therefore, the chair was not furniture. The chair was methodology. The chair was blue-ant|Blue Ant. She thought of the footage itself — seventy seconds, no music, no dialogue, no narration, just a sequence of images that did not feel like images, that felt like time held still.

Midpoint Reversal

Yokohama port. Fog. Sea. Memory. The cargo cranes stood in rows like sleeping herons, their red paint peeling under salt air. Cayce walked the fixie along the quay, the chain clicking a small rhythm against the chain-guard.

She found the original of the footage. The original was not data. The original was memory. Memory was culture, culture was refusal, and refusal was eternal.

But — and here she stopped, the fixie leaning against her hip, the red pedal out of reach of the fog — the footage was not memory. The footage was the refusal of memory. The footage was the shape memory left when memory was gone. The footage was the negative. Therefore, the footage was an x-ray of culture, the place where culture was missing. Refusal was culture, culture was refusal, and refusal was eternal. Therefore, the footage was the memory of refusal. Memory was culture, culture was refusal, refusal was me, and I was memory.

Her hands were cold. Although it was August. Because her body was a thermometer. Therefore, the footage was cold. The footage was a cold pattern. Cold patterns lasted the longest. The coldest patterns were the dead ones. Therefore, the footage was a dead pattern walking.

She thought of her father, the security specialist, his work in the Cold War — capital C, capital W — the projects that had placed objects in places that no one would find them. The footage was not placed. The footage was grown. Grown things were alive. Because they were alive, they could not be owned. Therefore, no one owned the footage. No one had ever owned the footage. However, hubertus-bigend|Bigend believed he could own it. Because Bigend believed he could own anything. Therefore, Bigend was wrong. Therefore, Cayce was hired.

Climax

"Cayce." Hollis said, the satellite delay gone now, the voice at human speed. "You found it."

"I found refusal," Cayce said. "Refusal was culture. Therefore, the footage was culture's memory. Memory is refusal."

However, she had not found the maker. The maker was a problem. Because the maker was a culture, and a culture was a thing that could not be located, only inhabited. The maker was a house the footage lived in. The footage was the house. The maker was the tenant. The tenant had moved out. The house remained. Therefore, the footage was a vacancy. Vacancies were the most valuable real estate. Because vacancies could be filled with anything. Therefore, blue-ant|Blue Ant wanted the footage. Therefore, the footage was a commodity. Therefore, the footage was dead. Dead things sold. Dead things had culture. Culture was refusal. Refusal was the dead.

Cayce pressed her palm flat against the fixie's red pedal. The paint was warm from the August sun. Because the paint was warm, the pedal was alive. Because the pedal was alive, the fixie was alive. Because the fixie was alive, the city was alive. Because the city was alive, the footage was alive. Therefore, she was not chasing a dead thing. Therefore, the dead were useful. Therefore, she was useful. Therefore, she was a coolhunter. Therefore, she was a tool. Therefore, she was a pattern. Patterns did not die. Therefore, she was a cold pattern walking. Therefore, she would last.

Resolution

The fog still swallowed Yokohama. The same fog. The same port. The same memory.

Cayce rode the fixie. The pedal was a red pedal. The pedal was mine. The pedal was culture's. Therefore, the pedal was memory. However, the pedal was also hers. Because it was hers, the pedal was a vessel. Because it was a vessel, it could carry cargo. Therefore, the fixie was a ship. Therefore, Yokohama was the home port. Therefore, Cayce was a courier. Couriers delivered patterns. Patterns were refusal. Refusal was eternal. Therefore, couriers were eternal. Therefore, Cayce was eternal.

The fixie rattled over a seam in the quay. Cayce's teeth clicked together. Although she had not eaten in eleven hours, she did not feel hungry. Because hunger was a brand. Because she was allergic to brands, she was allergic to hunger. Therefore, she was not hungry. Therefore, the footage was not hungry either. The footage was fed. The footage was fed on her. Because it was fed on her, the footage was a parasite. However, parasites were patterns. Because parasites were patterns, parasites were refusal. Therefore, the footage was refusal. Refusal was culture. Culture was her.

"Cayce." Hollis said, from somewhere. New York, probably. The signal had a different delay now. "Are you going back?"

"I go back as memory," Cayce said. "Memory is culture. Therefore, I go back."

The fog swallowed Cayce. Swallowing was refusal. Therefore, the fog was refusal. Refusal was culture, culture was me, I was fog, fog was Yokohama, Yokohama was memory, memory was culture, and culture was eternal. However, the fog also had a smell. Because the fog had a smell, the fog was real. Because the fog was real, Yokohama was real. Because Yokohama was real, the footage was real. Because the footage was real, the search was over. Therefore, the search was beginning. Therefore, she was not going back. Therefore, she was going forward. Therefore, forward was the same as back, when forward was the future of a pattern. Patterns did not have a future. Patterns had only a present. Therefore, she was present. Therefore, she was eternal. Therefore, the red pedal was eternal.

Yokohama is still a port. The fog still swallows. The footage still circulates.

Cayce's allergy still rings. Ringing is culture. Culture is memory. Memory is Yokohama. Although the search has ended, the finding has not. The finding is eternal. The eternal is refusal.
