ADR-0280 — the monotonicity-constrained curve search (Manus review's first choice).

WHY THIS EXPERIMENT EXISTS
The §5 attempt optimised mission completion directly and did not constrain the
per-encounter curve. It reached five of six targets but inverted that curve
(g4 7.2% per encounter while g6 sat at 99.3%), breaking ADR-0229's monotonic
non-increasing invariant. The Manus review ranked "soften the per-encounter curve
while keeping it monotonic" first, noting the invariant had to hold. That is a
different experiment from the one that failed, so it was run.

METHOD
`scripts/search_curve_constrained.py` coordinate-searches the 12 parameters
(6 HP caps + 6 damage bonuses) and rejects any candidate where
  (a) the HP caps are not monotonically non-decreasing, or
  (b) the measured per-encounter win rate is not monotonically non-increasing.
Each evaluation calls `scripts/curve_probe.ts`, which reports BOTH metrics in one
1.4s pass (per-encounter through `runSuite`, mission through `playRun`).
Objective: sum |mission completion - target| over the six grades.

RESULT — grades 1-4 hit, 5-6 impossible under the constraint
Per-encounter p: 100.0 / 100.0 / 100.0 / 47.0 / 40.7 / 0.0   (monotonic: yes)
Mission m:       100.0 /  95.0 /  81.8 / 48.0 /  0.0 /  0.0
Target:          100   /  95   /  80   / 50   / 25   / 10
Delta:             0.0 /   0.0 /  +1.8 / -2.0 / -25.0 / -10.0

Score improved from 125.1 to 29.8 (sum of absolute deltas). Grades 1-4 are all
inside the tool's 8pp tolerance; grade 2 lands exactly.

WHY 5 AND 6 CANNOT MOVE — the structural conflict, now proven
Monotonicity forces p5 <= p4. The search put p4 at 47.0%, so p5 <= 47.0%. But a
grade-5 mission is 7.4 encounters long: at 47% per encounter it finishes 0% of
the time. To get 25% completion over 7.4 encounters you need roughly 90% per
encounter -- which would exceed p4 and invert the curve again.

So the two curves are not reconcilable while mission length keeps growing with
grade. One of these has to give:
  - mission length at grades 5-6 (option B), or
  - the monotonicity invariant (option A, needs an ADR-0229 amendment), or
  - the targets at grades 5-6 (option D).

Side observation: with the softer curve, deaths at grades 1-3 are now 100% at the
boss and normal encounters never kill (see death location). The apex ICE is once
again the only wall at low grades, which is what the earlier boss fix addressed.
