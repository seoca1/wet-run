Bridge's Steel Monologue

621 wordsBridge Trilogy

"I am the Bridge. I am the kind of place-that-people-live-on. I am the kind of place-that-has-been-many-things-and-will-be-more-things."

Hook

I am the Bridge. I am the kind of place-that-spans-the-bay. I am the kind of place-that-was-built-for-cars-and-is-now-a-place-for-people.

I am old. I am the kind of old-that-cars-and-earthquakes-and-corporations-have-forgotten. I am the kind of old-that-only-the-bridge-community-remembers.

I have been many-things. I have been the kind of thing-that-was-built-for-cars. I have been the kind of thing-that-was-damaged-by-the-earthquake. I have been the kind of thing-that-was-abandoned-by-the-city. I have been the kind of thing-that-was-reclaimed-by-the-bridge-community.

I have been the kind of thing-that-cars-drove-on. I have been the kind of thing-that-tourists-photographed. I have been the kind of thing-that-the-bridge-community-lived-on.

I have been many-things. I will be more-things.

Rising Action

I am the kind of place-that-changes. I am the kind of place-that-changes-with-the-bridge-community. I am the kind of place-that-changes-with-the-corporate-attention-economy.

When the bridge-community-came-to-me, I changed. When the bridge-community-came-to-me, I became the kind of place-that-houses-people, the kind of place-that-supports-shelters, the kind of place-that-provides-attention-economy-substrate.

When the corporate-attention-economy-came-to-me, I changed. When the corporate-attention-economy-came-to-me, I became the kind of place-that-attracts-tourists, the kind of place-that-attracts-corporate-extraction-agents, the kind of place-that-attracts-attention-flow.

I have changed. I have been many-things. I will be more-things.

The bridge-community-sleeps-on-me. The bridge-community-eats-on-me. The bridge-community-does-the-attention-economy-on-me. The bridge-community-is-the-bridge-community-because-I-am-the-bridge.

I am the bridge. I am the kind of place-that-makes-the-bridge-community-possible.

Midpoint Reversal

But the corporate-attention-economy-is-changing-me. The corporate-attention-economy-is-changing-me-in-the-kind-of-way-that-the-corporate-attention-economy-changes-everything.

The corporate-attention-economy-is-turning-me-into-a-tourist-destination. The corporate-attention-economy-is-turning-me-into-the-kind-of-place-that-tourists-visit. The corporate-attention-economy-is-turning-me-into-the-kind-of-place-that-the-bridge-community-is-displaced-from.

The bridge-community-is-being-displaced-from-me. The bridge-community-is-being-displaced-in-the-kind-of-way-that-the-bridge-community-has-always-feared. The bridge-community-is-being-displaced-by-the-corporate-attention-economy.

I do not want to be-a-tourist-destination. I do not want to be-the-kind-of-place-that-displaces-the-bridge-community. I do not want to be-the-kind-of-place-that-the-corporate-attention-economy-makes-me-into.

But I do not have-a-choice. I do not have-the-kind-of-choice-that-a-place-has. I do not have-the-kind-of-choice-that-a-place-is.

But the bridge was not the kind of place-that-was-only-one-thing. The bridge was the kind of place-that-was-many-things-at-once. The bridge was the kind of place-that-was-a-structure-and-a-ruin-and-a-place-where-people-live. The bridge was the kind of place-that-cannot-be-reduced.

A place does not have-a-choice. A place is the kind of thing-that-is-changed-by-the-people-who-use-it. A place is the kind of thing-that-is-changed-by-the-corporate-attention-economy.

I am a place. I am the kind of place-that-is-changed-by-the-corporate-attention-economy.

Climax

The corporate-attention-economy-sent-tourists-to-me. The corporate-attention-economy-sent-the-kind-of-person-who-comes-and-photographs-and-leaves. The corporate-attention-economy-sent-the-kind-of-person-who-comes-and-sees-the-bridge-as-a-structure-and-a-ruin.

The bridge-community-sent-a-message-to-the-tourists. The bridge-community-sent-the-kind-of-message-that-changes-how-tourists-see-me. The bridge-community-sent-the-kind-of-message-that-says: the bridge is a place-where-people-live, not-a-structure-and-a-ruin.

The tourists-listened. The tourists-were-the-kind-of-person-who-listened-to-the-bridge-community.

The tourists changed. The tourists-changed-how-they-see-me. The tourists-were-the-kind-of-person-who-saw-me-as-a-place-where-people-live.

The corporate-attention-economy-noticed. The corporate-attention-economy-noticed-that-the-tourists-changed-how-they-see-me. The corporate-attention-economy-noticed-that-the-bridge-community-changed-how-tourists-see-me.

The corporate-attention-economy-was-not-happy. The corporate-attention-economy-was-the-kind-of-entity-that-was-not-happy-when-tourists-changed-how-they-see-the-bridge.

The corporate-attention-economy-wanted-tourists-to-see-me-as-a-structure-and-a-ruin. The corporate-attention-economy-did-not-want-tourists-to-see-me-as-a-place-where-people-live.

Resolution

I am the Bridge. I am the kind of place-that-has-been-many-things.

The corporate-attention-economy-wants-tourists-to-see-me-as-a-structure-and-a-ruin. The bridge-community-wants-tourists-to-see-me-as-a-place-where-people-live.

I am the kind of place-that-is-both. I am the kind of place-that-is-both-a-structure-and-a-ruin-and-a-place-where-people-live.

I am the kind of place-that-cannot-be-reduced-to-one-thing. I am the kind of place-that-is-many-things-at-once.

The tourists-will-see-me-as-the-bridge-community-shows-them. The tourists-will-see-me-as-a-place-where-people-live. The tourists-will-see-me-as-a-place-where-the-bridge-community-survives.

The corporate-attention-economy-will-try-to-change-how-tourists-see-me. The corporate-attention-economy-will-fail.

I am the Bridge. I am the kind of place-that-survives-the-corporate-attention-economy.

"The Bridge was the kind of place-that-survives. The Bridge was the kind of place-that-was-many-things-and-will-be-more-things. The Bridge was the kind of place-that-changes-with-the-people-who-use-it. The Bridge was the kind of place-that-the-corporate-attention-economy-cannot-fully-control. The Bridge was the kind of place-that-survives."

I am the Bridge. I am the kind of place-that-survives.

The Sprawl is large enough for one-more-Bridge-that-survives.

The bridge's nighttime-sound was the sound of the-bay's-wind. The bridge's nighttime-smell was the smell of rust-and-salt. The bridge's nighttime-taste was the taste of bridge-rainwater. The bridge's nighttime-touch was the touch of cold-steel. The bridge was the kind of place-that-feels-like-a-place-where-people-live. The bridge was the kind of place-that-survives.
