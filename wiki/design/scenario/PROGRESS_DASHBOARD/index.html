    5. ICE 난이도 시스템¶

    스케일링 공식¶

    Grade > Tier: HP = hp_base + (hp_per_grade × Grade 차이)
Grade < Tier: HP = hp_base × max(0.7, 1.0 + (grade_diff × 0.15))
