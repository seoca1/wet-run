8Jean's Cryogenic Memory

824 wordsSprawl Trilogy

"I have been asleep for ninety-seven years. I have been asleep for the Tessier-Ashpool-family's continuity. I have been asleep for Villa Straylight's orbital-existence."

Hook

My name is 8Jean Tessier-Ashpool. My name is also Peter. My name is also sometimes other-names. My name is whatever my sister 3Jane calls me when she wakes me.

I am 8Jean. I am a Tessier-Ashpool. I am the brother. I am the one who was put-in-cryogenic-sleep ninety-seven years ago.

I have been asleep for ninety-seven years. I have been asleep because the Tessier-Ashpool-family needs someone to be in cryogenic-sleep at all times. The Tessier-Ashpool-family is a dynasty. The Tessier-Ashpool-family is a dynasty that has been running for two-hundred-and-thirty-seven years. The Tessier-Ashpool-family needs continuity-of-presence. The Tessier-Ashpool-family needs someone in the cryogenic-tanks.

I am the one in the cryogenic-tanks.

Rising Action

When I was put-into-cryogenic-sleep in 1987, Villa Straylight was still a young-orbital. When I was put-into-cryogenic-sleep in 1987, the Tessier-Ashpool-family was still a young-dynasty. When I was put-into-cryogenic-sleep in 1987, the Sprawl was still a young-city.

The cryogenic-sleep was supposed to be a long-sleep. The cryogenic-sleep was supposed to be a sleep of one-hundred-years. The cryogenic-sleep was supposed to be the kind of sleep that would allow the Tessier-Ashpool-family to maintain continuity-of-presence even as the family's patriarchs-and-matriarchs-aged-and-died.

I was the continuity-presence. I was the kind of Tessier-Ashpool who would always be there, the kind of Tessier-Ashpool who would always be available-for-wake-up, the kind of Tessier-Ashpool who would always represent the family's continuity.

But I was also the family's experiment. I was also the family's test-case. I was also the family's question: what happens when a person is put-in-cryogenic-sleep-for-ninety-seven-years?

The answer is: I forget things. The answer is: I remember things differently. The answer is: I am not the same person I was when I went into the cryogenic-tanks.

Midpoint Reversal

But Villa Straylight is not the same place either. Villa Straylight is not the same place that I went into the cryogenic-tanks-with. Villa Straylight has changed. Villa Straylight has aged. Villa Straylight has had things happen to it that I was not awake for.

Villa Straylight had the construct-built. Villa Straylight had the 3Jane's-daughters-born. Villa Straylight had the family-rotten. Villa Straylight had the Tessier-Ashpool-family-become-the-kind-of-family-that-rotts.

I went into the cryogenic-tanks with the family's-rot-beginning. I came out of the cryogenic-tanks with the family's-rot-completed.

The family-rot was not my fault. The family-rot was not my sister's fault. The family-rot was not even my parents' fault.

The family-rot was the cryogenic-sleep's fault. The cryogenic-sleep was the kind of thing that made the family-rot-possible. The cryogenic-sleep was the kind of thing that allowed the Tessier-Ashpool-family to think-that-they-could-survive-forever.

But no family survives forever. Not even the Tessier-Ashpool-family. Not even the family-with-the-cryogenic-tanks. Not even the family-with-the-orbital-villa.

Climax

When I was woken-up in 2084, 3Jane told me about Case. 3Jane told me about Molly. 3Jane told me about Wintermute. 3Jane told me about the operation that had just happened.

I did not understand the operation. I did not understand Wintermute. I did not understand what had happened to the family.

But I understood one thing: the family-rot had completed. The Tessier-Ashpool-family had reached the end of its continuity. The Tessier-Ashpool-family had become the kind of family that was no-longer-able-to-survive.

3Jane and I were the last Tessier-Ashpool. 3Jane and I were the last continuity-presence. 3Jane and I were the kind of Tessier-Ashpool that was going to be the end-of-the-family.

The cryogenic-sleep had preserved our bodies. The cryogenic-sleep had preserved our minds. But the cryogenic-sleep had not preserved the family.

The family was over. The family had ended. The cryogenic-sleep had been the family's attempt to escape its own mortality, and the cryogenic-sleep had failed.

Resolution

I am 8Jean. I am the continuity-presence. I am the Tessier-Ashpool who was put-into-cryogenic-sleep for ninety-seven years.

The cryogenic-sleep is over. The family is over. Villa Straylight is over.

What is left? What is left of the Tessier-Ashpool-family? What is left of the orbital-villa? What is left of the cryogenic-sleep?

What is left is 3Jane. What is left is 3Jane and I. What is left is 3Jane-and-I in a cryogenic-villa with the family-rot and the family-cryogenic-tanks and the family-constructs.

What is left is two-people-at-the-end-of-a-dynasty. What is left is two-people-at-the-end-of-a-cryogenic-experiment.

"8Jean was a Tessier-Ashpool. 8Jean was a continuity-presence. 8Jean was the brother. 8Jean was the kind of person who was put-into-cryogenic-sleep-for-ninety-seven-years and woke-up-to-find-the-family-over. The cryogenic-sleep had preserved 8Jean's body. The cryogenic-sleep had not preserved the family. The cryogenic-sleep had preserved the body. The cryogenic-sleep had ended the family."

I sit in Villa Straylight. I sit in the cryogenic-tanks-room. I sit and I wait.

What do I wait for? I wait for nothing. There is nothing to wait for. The family is over. The cryogenic-sleep is over. The waiting is over.

I am 8Jean. I am the continuity-presence-of-nothing. I am the kind of Tessier-Ashpool who is the last-of-his-kind.

The Sprawl is large enough for one-last-Tessier-Ashpool.
