Hook

This was the first thing Chevette Washington knew when she woke, on the night of the night that would become the night she would meet a man with a package, on the western span of the Bay Bridge. The fog had arrived from somewhere — from the Pacific, perhaps, or from the Bay's cold water, or from the cold that lived between the cables of the western span and never left. Chevette did not know where the fog had arrived from. Chevette did not need to know. Chevette lived in the fog. The fog was Chevette's medium. The medium was the bridge's. The bridge was the Bay's. The Bay was what the bridge was over. Therefore, the fog was what the bridge was over. Therefore, the fog was what Chevette was over.

The fog smelled of salt. Salt was what the fog smelled of. The smell of the fog was salt. The salt was the Pacific's. The Pacific was to the west. The west was where the fog had arrived from. Therefore, the salt was the west's.

The salt also smelled of rust. Rust was what the salt also smelled of. The rust was the bridge's. The bridge was rusting. The rusting was the bridge's. The bridge was also the Bay's. The Bay was also rusting. The rusting was the Bay's. The Bay was also the Pacific's. The Pacific was also rusting. Therefore, everything was rusting. Chevette did not mind the rust. Chevette was under the rust. The rust was over Chevette. Therefore, Chevette was under.

Midpoint Reversal

The man was different from the fog. Different from the fog was not fog. Not-fog was the man. Therefore, the man was not the fog.

Climax
