Marly did not touch it. She had learned, in the three months she had worked for Virek, that Virek preferred to touch first. The touch of Virek was the touch of a man who had been, in his own words, "much rebuilt." The much-rebuilt-ness of Virek was the much-rebuilt-ness of a man whose expensive invalidity had made him a particular kind of curator.
