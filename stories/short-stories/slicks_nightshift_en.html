Slick's Nightshift

An Ex-Cowboy at the Wage-Slave Bar

297 wordsSprawl Trilogy

The bar was in Ninsei. The bar was not a bar. The bar was a wage-slave pickup joint. The bar was the kind of place where a man could be hired for a few hours to do work that could not be traced.

Case had worked there for four years. Case had worked there under the name Slick Henry. Slick Henry was not a name anyone in the matrix would have recognized.

Tonight the bar was quiet.

Case was wiping the counter when a man came in. The man wore a good suit. The man's suit was too good for Ninsei.

"Slick," the man said.

Case did not look up.

"Who's asking."

"I'm asking. I'm asking for a friend."

Case looked up.

The man's face was unfamiliar. The man's accent was from Sprawl-side. The man's eyes had been replaced.

"Sit down," Case said.

The man sat.

"My friend has a small job. The job is on the BAMA side. The job is for a man who used to be a cowboy."

"There aren't any cowboys in Chiba."

"There used to be one."

Case finished wiping the counter.

"That was a long time ago."

"My friend is willing to pay."

"My friend is willing to pay a lot."

Case set the rag down.

"I don't do that work anymore."

"Everyone who has ever done it says that."

"Not everyone has ever done it for who I did it for."

The man looked at him. The man looked at him with eyes that were not eyes.

"Then what do you do?"

"I do what I'm doing. I wipe the counter."

The man stood up.

"My friend will be back."

"Let him."

The man left.

Case finished wiping the counter. Case put the rag under the bar. Case went back to his small apartment. Case did not take the job.

In the morning, his hands were still steady. In the morning, his hands were still the hands of a man who had once been a cowboy. In the morning, he was still not one.
