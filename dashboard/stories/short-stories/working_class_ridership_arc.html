Working Class Ridership Arc

"The Sprawl was the era's transportation network for working-class data. Every operative who rode it became part of its ridership — part of the apparatus that the corporate-state depended on to keep itself moving. The ridership was the era's invisible labor force."

— extended from *Neuromancer*, the ridership-procedural scene

Hook

The ridership had *a member*.

This was the first thing. The ridership was the *era's working-class substrate*. The working-class substrate was the *era's*. The era's working-class substrate was the *ridership*. The ridership had *a member*.

The member was the *ridership's*. The ridership had a member. *Therefore*, the ridership was *membered*. *Therefore*, the ridership had *a member*.

The transportation was the *Sprawl*. The Sprawl was the *era's transportation*. The era's Sprawl was the *transportation*. *Therefore*, the Sprawl was the *data-operator*.

The data-operator was the *Sprawl's member*. The Sprawl's member was the *data-operator*. The member was *the Sprawl's ridership*.

The *ridership* was the *Sprawl's*. The Sprawl had *a ridership*. The ridership was the *Sprawl's*.

Rising Action

The ridership was *invisible*. The invisibility was the *ridership's*. The ridership was *invisible to the era*. The era could not see the ridership. *Therefore*, the ridership was *invisible*.

The invisible was the *era's working-class*. The era's working-class was the *invisible*. *Therefore*, the era's working-class was *the ridership*.

The *ridership* was the *working-class*. The working-class was *the ridership*. The ridership was the *working-class's transportation*.

The transportation was the *data*. The data was *the transportation*. The transportation was the *data's*.

The data was the *era's invisible labor*. The invisible labor was the *data's*. The data was the era's labor.

The ridership was *the era's data*. The data was the era's ridership. *Therefore*, the ridership was *the era's*.

The era was *the ridership*.

Midpoint Reversal

The data-operator *had a route*. The route was the data-operator's. The operator had a route. *Therefore*, the route was *the operator's*.

The *route* was the *operator's daily transportation*. The daily transportation was the route's. The daily was the *route's*. The route was the operator's daily.

The operator's *daily* was the *era's working-class labor*. The daily was the operator's. The daily was the era's. *Therefore*, the operator's daily was the *era's working-class*.

The paying was *the Sprawl*. The Sprawl was *the paying*. The Sprawl paid. *Therefore*, the Sprawl was *the era's payment*.

But — and here the data-operator paused, mid-route, at a transit hub between L-5 and Chiba's Narita-reclamation sector — *but* the *payment* was the *operator's cost*. The cost was the *payment's*. The payment was the *cost*.

The *cost* was the operator's *route*. The route was the *cost's*. The route was *the operator's cost*.

The operator paid *for the route*. The paying-for-the-route was the operator's. The operator paid. *Therefore*, the operator paid *for the route*.

The *paying* was the *operator's daily*. The daily was the *paying*. The daily was *the operator's payment*.

The *operator* paid the *era* for the *route* to the *Sprawl*. The paying-the-era was the operator's. The era was *the paying's destination*. The route was the Sprawl's. *Therefore*, the operator paid *the era* *for the route* *to the Sprawl*.

The era *received* the *payment*. The receiving was the era's. The payment was received. *Therefore*, the era received *the payment*.

The receiving was *the corporate-state's*. The corporate-state was the era's *receiving*. The era was the corporate-state's. *Therefore*, the receiving was the *corporate-state's*.

The corporate-state *received* the *operator's daily*. The corporate-state received. The corporate-state received *the operator*. *Therefore*, the corporate-state received *the operator's daily*.

The operator's *daily* was the corporate-state's *receiving*. The daily was the operator's. The receiving was the corporate-state's. *Therefore*, the operator's daily was the *corporate-state's receipt*.

Climax

The operator *rode* the Sprawl. The riding was the operator's. The operator rode. *Therefore*, the operator *was riding*.

The *riding* was the *era's transportation*. The era's transportation was the *riding*. The riding was the era's. *Therefore*, the operator rode *the era's transportation*.

The *ceiling* was the *era's*. The era's ceiling was the *L-5 corridor*. The era's L-5 corridor was the *ceiling*.

The operator *rode* the *L-5*. The riding-the-L-5 was the operator's. The L-5 was *ridden*. *Therefore*, the operator *rode the L-5*.

Resolution

The *operator* *had arrived*. The arrival was the operator's. The operator had arrived. *Therefore*, the operator *was the arrived*.

The *arrived* was the *era's*. The era had arrived. *Therefore*, the era was *the arrived*.

The era's ceiling was the *operator's daily*. The daily was the operator's. The era's ceiling was the *daily*.

Epilogue

The *L-5 corridor* would *remain*. The remaining was the L-5's. The corridor would remain. *Therefore*, the L-5 was *forever*.

*Derivative short-story for the Sprawl Trilogy. The working-class ridership is the era's invisible labor force — data-operators who ride the Sprawl's corporate-state apparatus as their daily transportation, paying for the privilege of being corporate-state labor. This short-story fills a moment when a data-operator completes a daily route between L-5 and Chiba's Narita-reclamation sector, the routine that constitutes both the era's invisible labor and the corporate-state's invisible receipt. The "*X is the Y*" register is preserved as the primary voice; the operator's daily is the era's transportation, the era's labor, the corporate-state's receipt.*
