Sil's Last Delivery

A Veteran Runner Carries the Box Across the Long Bridge

268 wordsSprawl Trilogy

The box was in her left hand. The box was in her left hand because the left hand was the hand that had been wearing the glove. The glove had been Olf's glove.

Olf had been a runner. Olf had been a runner before the runners had been a runner. Olf had been her teacher for nine years.

The runners had been a runner for as long as the city had been a city.

The box had been the box for the runners. The runners had been a runner for the box. The box had been the box for the man who had been a man.

The man was Wigan. Wigan had been a man for as long as the runners had been a runner. The runners had been a runner for the man.

Sil had been running for nine years. The runners had been running for nine years. The running had been the running for the man.

She walked south. The city was the city. The city had been the city for the runners.

The bridge was the bridge. The bridge had been the bridge for the runners. The runners had been the runners for the bridge.

The box was the box. The box was the box that Wigan had been waiting for. Wigan had been waiting for the box.

Sil had not been waiting. Sil had been running. The running had been the running for as long as the box had been the box.

She turned the corner. The corner was a corner of the city. The city had been the city for the runners.

In the morning, the box would be at Wigan's office. Wigan would open the box. Wigan would know what the box had been.

Sil did not need to know what the box had been. The box had been the box. The box had been the box for the runners.

She kept walking.
