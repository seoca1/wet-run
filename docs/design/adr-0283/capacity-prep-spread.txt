Five grades inside the 8pp tolerance; grade 6 is +8.1pp on 72 runs, i.e.
inside the noise. The wall that healing, curve softening, deck strength and
the invariants all failed to break is broken at grades 5 and 6.

Response is steep, not gradual: g5 at +200 is 6.9% and at +270 is 30.6%, so
the table is a measured edge rather than a comfortable middle.

The table is NOT monotone (g5 270 > g6 175) and that is deliberate: grade 5
is 7.4 encounters against grade 6's 7.3, and its apex ICE is neuromancer
(armour 9) against aleph (armour 7). Unlike the ICE HP cap there is no
invariant on a player stat.
