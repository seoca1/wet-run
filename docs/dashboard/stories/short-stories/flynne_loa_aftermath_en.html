After the LOA: Flynne in the Trailer

Origin: Flynne dealing with the LOA aftermath

1254 wordsPeripheral

The trailer was quiet in a way that trailers are not usually quiet. Trailers are usually quiet in the way that everything is quiet in a place that has been lived in for years, which is to say quiet in a way that has sounds in it, the sounds of the place remembering the things, of the pipes ticking, of the refrigerator cycling, of the floor under the carpet holding the weight of the people who have walked on it. This trailer was quiet in a different way. This trailer was quiet in the way that a place is quiet after something has happened to it, the way a kitchen is quiet after the meal has been served, the way a clinic is quiet after the patient has gone home, the way a body is quiet after the body has stopped doing what the body was doing.

The pod was still in the living room.

The pod was open. The seam of the pod was parted. The inside of the pod was still warm from me. The needles of the pod were still indented with the shape of me.

I was sitting at the kitchen table. Burton was not in the trailer. Burton was at the medical center in Knoxville, where the medical center was a kind of low brick building with parking spots marked out for ambulances that no longer came to that medical center for the kind of emergency that the parking spots were originally marked out for. Burton had gone to the medical center to be looked at by someone who knew what to look at. Burton had gone because the LOA had done something to Burton that needed to be looked at by someone who knew what to look at.

The LOA had done a thing to me too.

The LOA had done a thing to me that did not need to be looked at by someone who knew what to look at, because the LOA had done a thing to me that I was going to look at myself, in the trailer, on the kitchen table, with the pod open in the living room and Burton at the medical center in Knoxville and the refrigerator cycling in the corner.

The thing the LOA had done to me was this:

I could remember two bodies at once.

I could remember the body I was in, which was the body that was mine, which was the body that was sitting at the kitchen table in the trailer in Clanton. I could also remember the body that was not mine, which was the body that I had been wearing in London at the time of the LOA, which was the body that I had been wearing when the LOA had done whatever the LOA had done.

The body that was not mine was not in the trailer.

The body that was not mine was in London. The body that was not mine was being looked at by the people who looked at bodies that were not theirs in London. The body that was not mine was not coming back to the trailer, because the body that was not mine had been used up by the LOA in a way that I did not fully understand.

I could remember two bodies.

The body that was mine was the body that was sitting at the kitchen table. The body that was not mine was the body that had been in London. The two bodies were not the same body. The two bodies had been the same body, by my hand, while I had been operating the body that was not mine from the body that was mine.

Now the two bodies were two bodies again.

The body that was not mine was in London, being looked at. The body that was mine was in Clanton, being me.

I picked up the coffee mug from the kitchen table. The coffee mug was the one that said "Maqsood's Quality Meats." The coffee in the coffee mug was cold. The skin on the coffee was the color of a contact lens.

I drank the cold coffee.

The cold coffee tasted like the future, except that the cold coffee was not from the future. The cold coffee was from this morning. The cold coffee had been made this morning by me, in the trailer, with the pod open in the living room.

The refrigerator cycled.

The pod was warm in the living room.

The trailer was quiet in the way that trailers are quiet after something has happened to them.

I could remember two bodies.

The body that was not mine had been in a park in London when the LOA happened. The park in London had been the kind of park that I had only ever seen in London parks, which is a park that has been a park for a long time and that has been a park in a way that I could feel in the body that was not mine, the way that a body that has been in a park for a long time can feel that the park has been a park for a long time.

The park in London had been a park in a way that the trailer in Clanton was not a park in.

The trailer in Clanton had been a trailer for the years that we had lived in it. The trailer in Clanton had been a trailer in a way that the park in London had been a park in. The trailer had been a trailer long enough to have the weight of a trailer.

The body that was not mine had been in the park when the LOA had done the thing. The body that was not mine had been holding a thing in the body that was not mine at the time. The thing that the body that was not mine had been holding had been a thing that I had been told to hold.

I could not remember what the thing had been.

I could remember that I had been holding it. I could remember that the thing had been a thing that I had been told to hold. I could not remember what the thing had been.

The refrigerator cycled.

I drank more of the cold coffee.

The cold coffee was from this morning. The cold coffee had been made by me. The cold coffee was the only cold coffee in the trailer. The cold coffee was the only thing in the trailer that had been made by me this morning.

The pod in the living room had been used by me this morning.

The pod in the living room had been used by me this morning and the needles of the pod had been indented with the shape of me this morning and the body that was not mine had been in a park in London this morning and the LOA had done the thing to the body that was not mine this morning.

This morning had been a long morning.

I picked up the phone.

The phone did not ring.

I put the phone down.

I sat at the kitchen table.

I waited for Burton to come back from the medical center in Knoxville with whatever it was that Burton was bringing back from the medical center in Knoxville about the thing that the LOA had done to Burton.

The refrigerator cycled.

The trailer was quiet.

I could remember two bodies.
