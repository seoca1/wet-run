The Pairing Audited

"The heretic audits the trauma-squads / cortex-hound pairing. The trauma-squads is the era's brain-damage-management service class. The cortex-hound is the era's class-enforcement disabling technology. The trauma-squads and the cortex-hound are paired — the squad manages what the hound disables. The pairing is the era's late-Capitalist class-enforcement mechanism."
