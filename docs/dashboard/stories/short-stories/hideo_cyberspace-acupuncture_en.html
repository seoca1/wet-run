Hideo's Cyberspace Acupuncture

962 wordsSprawl Trilogy

"The boy had damaged his own nervous system. The boy's damage was not a mistake. The boy's damage was the cost of the boy's gift."

Hook

I am Hideo. I am a cyberspace-cowboy of the old-school. I came up through the Chiba City underground, the kind of underground that no longer exists, the kind of underground that the zaibatsu-corporations have since paved over with neon-lit-real-estate-developments.

I have been a cyberspace-cowboy for twenty years. I have trained three generations of cowboys. The first generation learned on hardware I built with my own hands. The second generation learned on hardware I bought from the zaibatsu. The third generation—the boy Case—learns on hardware I bought from the zaibatsu and damaged with my own hands.

The boy Case damaged his own nervous system. The boy Case damaged his nervous system because the boy Case tried to steal from his employers. The boy Case damaged his nervous system because the boy Case thought he was smarter than the people who owned him.

The boy Case was wrong about that.

Rising Action

I have been teaching the boy Case for three years. The teaching has not been the kind of teaching that happens in classrooms. The teaching has been the kind of teaching that happens in the small-rooms above the Chiba City massage-parlors, the kind of rooms where the air smells of incense and antiseptic and the neon-light from the street below casts the kind of light that makes everything look like a film-noir.

The teaching has been acupuncture. The teaching has been the placement of needles along the boy's nervous-system, the placement of needles that allow the boy to enter the matrix with the right-shade-of-blue.

The matrix is not a place. The matrix is a consensus-hallucination. The matrix is what every computer in the Sprawl shows you when you jack in. The matrix is the world-as-data, the world-as-numbers, the world-as-the-kind-of-blue-that-is-not-the-blue-of-the-sky.

The boy Case wants to live in the matrix. The boy Case wants to live in the matrix because the boy Case cannot live in the Sprawl. The boy Case cannot live in the Sprawl because the boy Case's body is too damaged, the boy Case's nervous-system is too raw, the boy Case's mind cannot bear the world-as-it-is.

Midpoint Reversal

But the boy Case was not the only student I have taught. The boy Case was not the only student I have damaged. I have taught students who became great. I have taught students who became cowboys. I have taught students who entered the matrix and stayed in the matrix and lost themselves in the matrix.

But the boy Case was different. The boy Case was different because the boy Case did not want to enter the matrix. The boy Case wanted to LIVE in the matrix. The boy Case wanted the matrix to be the boy's permanent-residence, the boy's permanent-home, the boy's permanent-self.

The boy's desire was a problem. The boy's desire was a problem because the matrix is not a place where a body can live. The matrix is a place where a mind can visit, can work, can perform, can survive-for-a-few-hours. The matrix is not a place where a body can stay.

I could not teach the boy Case that. The boy Case would not learn that. The boy Case's employers-learned-it-for-him.

Climax

When the boy Case came to me after his employers had damaged his nervous system, I knew what had happened. The boy Case's employers had used a Russian mycotoxin. The boy Case's employers had used the mycotoxin to prevent the boy Case from jacking-in. The boy Case's employers had used the mycotoxin as a punishment, the kind of punishment that the Sprawl's underworld administers to its unfaithful-cowboys.

The boy Case could not jack-in. The boy Case could not enter the matrix. The boy Case could not live in the blue-shade-of-everything.

The boy Case wanted to die. The boy Case wanted to die because the boy Case could not live without the matrix.

I could not fix the boy Case's nervous system. I could not give the boy Case back his matrix. I could only give the boy Case the place to recover from his disappointment, the place where the boy Case could wait for someone to come along with a better offer.

Resolution

I am Hideo. I am a cyberspace-cowboy of the old-school. I have taught three generations of cowboys. The third generation—the boy Case—will not be the last generation.

There will be other cowboys. There will be other students. There will be other boys who want to live in the matrix. There will be other boys who want to damage their own nervous systems for the privilege of jacking-in.

I will teach them. I will give them the acupuncture. I will give them the access. I will give them the blue-shade-of-everything.

But I will not give them the matrix. The matrix is not mine to give. The matrix belongs to the corporations, to the AI's, to the weather, to the world.

The matrix is a place I have visited. The matrix is a place I have worked. The matrix is a place I have lived-in.

But the matrix is not a place where a body can live. The matrix is not a place where a body can stay.

The boy Case learned that the hard way. The boy Case's employers-taught the boy Case the hard way.

I just watched.

"The acupuncture was a gift. The cyberspace was a gift. The nervous-system-damage was the cost. The boy Case had learned the cost. The boy Case would learn what came next. The boy Case's story was just beginning."

I put the needles away. I cleaned the small-room above the Chiba City massage-parlor. I waited for the next cowboy.

The next cowboy would come. The next cowboy would always come. The Sprawl's appetite for cyberspace-cowboys is never-ending.
