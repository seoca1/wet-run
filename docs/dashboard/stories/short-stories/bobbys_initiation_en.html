Bobby's Initiation

A Child of the BAMA Fringe Meets the Loa

285 wordsSprawl Trilogy

The loft was small. The loft was on the BAMA fringe. The loft had no windows.

Bobby sat on the floor. Bobby was thirteen. Bobby had been working the deck for nine months.

Tonight the deck was showing him something new.

A construct was running a long thread through the lower matrix. The construct was thin. The construct was bright. The construct was following a path Bobby did not recognize.

Bobby followed the construct.

The construct came to a node that was not a node. The node was a place in the matrix where the rules did not apply. The construct entered the node.

Inside the node was a small square room. Inside the square room was a small figure.

The small figure was not a person. The small figure was not a construct. The small figure was a voodoo, the kind that had been reported but never confirmed.

"Hello," Bobby said.

The small figure turned.

"You are the new one," the small figure said.

"I am Bobby."

"You are the new one."

The voodoo looked at him for a long time.

"You will do," the voodoo said.

Bobby did not understand. Bobby said so.

"You will do for the Loa. The Loa are watching. The Loa have been watching. The Loa want you."

Bobby was afraid. Bobby was excited. Bobby had never met a Loa.

"What do the Loa want?"

The voodoo smiled.

"The Loa want what they have always wanted. The Loa want a person who is willing to be present."

Bobby said nothing.

"Stay with us," the voodoo said. "Stay with us for a while."

Bobby stayed.

In the morning, his mother would not understand why he had been sitting on the floor all night.

In the morning, Bobby would not be able to explain.

The deck hummed.
