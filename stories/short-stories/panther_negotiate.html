Panther Moderns' Retainer

*"The Falconer's lounge sits below street level, the way the lounges in Chiba always sit — a stairwell down from the rain ..."*

— Gibson, Sprawl Trilogy

Setup: The Falconer

The Falconer's lounge sits below street level, the way the lounges in Chiba always sit — a stairwell down from the rain and a payphone that never answers. The bass drum sits in the corner like a coffin that has learned to hold a different kind of body. Finn has the cuttle-cassette angled under the bar. The runner eats shabu and pretends not to notice.

Rising: The Panther Strut

The Panther Moderns do not walk into the Chatō. The Panther Moderns do not walk at all. They arrive the way weather arrives — by being there when you look up. Z-surge jacket. Chiba-strut calibrated to Sense/Net's preferred hiring profile. A face workshopped into the corporate employer's idea of a cowboy. The Moderns are paid for looking the part. Looking the part is the retainer.

Climax: The Tessier-Ashpool Core

The runner jacks into a Tessier-Ashpool core designed to look, to ICE, like a Sense/Net extract. The core is a sandwich: Sense/Net's outer signature wrapping Tessier-Ashpool's inner ICE. The ICE does not see the difference. The ICE was never built to see the difference. The corporate employer pays for not being seen. The Moderns pay for looking the part. The runner pays for playing it. Three payers, three roles, one extraction.

Resolution: The Retainer Run

Retainer-class work is corporate extractions that want counter-operative cosmetic. The corporate extractions do not pay the Moderns to look like Moderns on the run. They pay the Moderns to look like the corporate employer's chosen-cowboy on the run. The Panther Moderns' retainer offer is the corporate employer's stylistic. The runner now wears the right Z-surge jacket, walks with the Chiba-strut, jacks into the Tessier-Ashpool core. The ICE doesn't see the difference because the difference, in the era's late-Capitalist retainer logic, is the same thing.

Epilogue: The Late-Capitalist

The Panther Moderns vanish back into the consensual hallucination of Ninsei's neon. The runner eats the rest of the shabu. Finn counts the credit under the table. The retainer offer was a corporate employer's stylistic. The corporate employer paid for the stylistic. The stylistic was the corporate employer's signature on a work order that no one will ever be able to file in any corporate archive. This is what the era's late-Capitalist retainer logic looks like when the era has stopped pretending it is anything other than a costume party for the working-class to wear.

확장 에필로그: The Continuation

The Falconer's lounge empties the way lounges in Chiba empty: slowly, with the reluctance of people who have been in a place long enough to know the place is not the place they thought the place was when they walked in. Finn stays after the runner leaves. Finn stays because Finn is the kind of fixer who stays. The kind of fixer who stays is the kind of fixer who counts credit under the table and does not ask the runner what the runner brought back. The runner brought back what the runner brought back. What the runner brought back is what the Panther Moderns paid the runner to bring back. The Panther Moderns paid the runner what the Panther Moderns paid the runner because the Panther Moderns have been paid for this kind of work for twenty years. Twenty years is the era's retainer-class arithmetic. Twenty years is the arithmetic the era uses when the era needs a body that does not belong to the body the era wants seen. The body's owner is irrelevant. The body's owner has been irrelevant since the era's late-Capitalist retainer logic decided that the owner of the body and the body itself are different commodities, bought and sold on different markets, with different currencies and different interest rates. The body walks into the rain. The body's owner walks into the rain on a different street in a different city at a different hour. The rain is the same. The rain is what the era gives every body to walk into. The rain is what the era gives every owner to walk into. The era has stopped pretending the rain is anything other than the era's accounting.

확장 코다: The Walk

The body walks out into the next morning. The next morning is what the era's late-Capitalist class-substance produces as the era's working-class operational image. The operational image is the morning. The morning is the body. The body is the morning. The body walks through the morning the way the morning walks through the body. The walk is the era's class-substance. The class-substance is the walk. The walk is what the working-class-matrix-operator does when the working-class-matrix-operator has finished the run and the run has finished the era and the era has finished the working-class-matrix-operator. The working-class-matrix-operator is what the era's late-Capitalist class-substance produces as the era's working-class-matrix-operator. The class-substance is the working-class-matrix-operator. The working-class-matrix-operator is the class-substance. The class-substance is the era. The era is the morning. The morning is the body. The body is the run. The run is the era. The era continues. The era has continued since the era was the era. The era will continue until the era is not the era. The era is not the era when the working-class-matrix-operator stops being the working-class-matrix-operator. The working-class-matrix-operator has not stopped being the working-class-matrix-operator. The working-class-matrix-operator is the working-class-matrix-operator. The working-class-matrix-operator walks through the morning. The morning is the era. The era is the walk. The walk is the working-class-matrix-operator. The working-class-matrix-operator walks.
