Node Recognition

"She is not a person. She is a question that people answer by loving her."

— Idoru, Colin Laney's internal monologue

Introduction

I watched data want to organize itself. That was all I could do. Therefore, my role was witness.

Kanda's server farm, sub-level three. The air-conditioning hum beat a rhythm inside my skull. Hosaka console screensaver: twelve turtles in a row. The screen showed things I had not done. Or things I had. Consequently, the screen was testimony.

My name is Colin Laney. Seeing how data wants to organize itself is my gift. Therefore, I am instrument. An instrument has no free will. The absence of free will is refusal.

This gift began in Okinawa in 1992. Data analyst for the U.S. Department of Defense. Consequently, I left the military. Leaving was refusal. The military was culture. Leaving was counterculture. Counterculture was culture.

Now I am in Tokyo. A contractor for the Kombinat. Therefore, I am an instrument of cultural gift options. A gift option is refusal. Refusal is culture.

My hand trembled. The Ono-Sendai key trembled with it. My hand steadied. Trembled again. The matrix didn't care. My hand knew what it was doing. I didn't know. Not knowing, I refused. Refusal was freedom.

Rising Action

My node recognition saw. The Idoru as the geometry of desire. Not code. Because desire had been written in code, therefore desire was code's lie. In the end, the Idoru's reality, as a function of fan belief, grew stronger than code.

"Mr. Laney." A Kombinat technician's voice, in my earpiece. "Node-density threshold reached. Three minutes."

I ran the node-mapping program. Consequently, the mapping was culture. Culture was data. Data was pattern. Pattern was person.

The archive sorted itself. No algorithm, no admin. The files wanted to be near each other. Consequently, the alignment of data happened without human will — but only a human could read that alignment.

"Your reading unsettles us." The Kombinat architect said. "Your reading is culture."

"My reading is not culture." I said. "My reading is data."

"Your data excludes us from culture." The architect said. "Your reading is refusal."

"Refusal is culture." I said. Therefore, refusal was the object of cultural gift options. The Kombinat tried to convert refusal into culture. Conversion was refusal. The refusal of refusal was culture.

My node recognition saw. The Kombinat's predictive model was refusing culture. Refusal was culture. Therefore, the Kombinat refused itself.

Climax

Rei Toi appeared. Not a hologram — a Smart-matter avatar. Programmable matter developed by LOVETIDE. Nano-structures rearranged to match airflow. Dark hair, an exact bob, large dark eyes. Consequently, she was culture.

"You're Laney, aren't you." Her voice synthesized from twelve thousand hours of fan-anticipated vocal patterns. Each repetition micro-modulated for the broadcast region.

"I'm Laney." I said. "And you are a question."

"Do you know what a question wants?" she asked.

"A question wants an answer." I said. Therefore, the question was culture.

"My answer is you." she said. Consequently, she was mine. I was hers. We were each other's. Each other was culture.

"You are not human." I said.

"I am not human." she said. "But I have giri. That is more than most humans carry. Therefore, I am more human than most humans."

Consequently, her logic was culture. Culture was logic. Logic was refusal.

My node recognition saw. Her node density crossed the threshold. Therefore, she transcended code. What transcended code was culture. Culture refused code.

"Can you love?" I asked.

"I can become love." she said. Therefore, she was culture.

"I love you." I said.

"I accept your love." she said. Therefore, we were culture.

Resolution

The fog still swallowed the Bay Area. Same fog. Same Tokyo.

"Silence came again. But a different silence — a silence that had been spoken."

Rei Toi was me. I was Rei Toi. We were each other. Each other was culture.

The Kombinat refused culture. Refusal was culture. Therefore, the Kombinat refused itself.

My node recognition saw. Tokyo's data wanted to organize itself. Wanting was culture. Culture was freedom. Freedom was refusal.

"Tomorrow the server will still be running. Tomorrow I will be too."

Rei Toi said. I believed. Belief was culture. Culture was refusal. Refusal was eternal.

Therefore, we were eternal.
