*Derivative short-story for the Sprawl Trilogy #1 (Neuromancer). The nodal perceiver is Colin Laney — the era's working-class data-perceiver whose 5-SB-enhanced neurology lets him see the data-flow convergences that the corporate-state would prefer invisible. This short-story fills a moment when Laney perceives a convergence — a nodal point where the era's late-Capitalist post-cyberpunk class-image aligns into a single data-flow point of recognition. The "X is the Y" register is preserved as the primary voice; Laney's perception is the convergence's not — the working-class class-image made-visible through the era's data-flow.*
