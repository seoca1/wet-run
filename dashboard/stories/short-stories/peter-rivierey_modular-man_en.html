Peter Rivierey's Modular Man

1225 wordsSprawl Trilogy

"My body is not a single body. My body is a series of bodies. My body is a modular-system. My body is whatever I need it to be."

Hook

My name is Peter Rivierey. My name was Peter Rivierey before Virek. My name will be Peter Rivierey after Virek. My name is the only thing about me that has not been modular-ized, not been replaced, not been upgraded, not been made into the kind of thing that Virek needed me to be.

My body is not a single-body. My body is a series-of-bodies. My body is a modular-system. My body is whatever Virek needed me to be, the kind of body that could infiltrate any-environment, the kind of body that could survive any-climate, the kind of body that could pass through any-checkpoint.

Virek did not just modular-ize my body. Virek modular-ized my mind. Virek modular-ized my memories. Virek modular-ized everything about me except my name.

I have pieces of memory that do not fit together. I have pieces of memory that are not mine. I have pieces of memory that belong to other-people who were modular-ized before me.

I have pieces of memory that are Marly's. I have pieces of memory that are Bobby's. I have pieces of memory that are Angie Mitchell's. I have pieces of memory that are the Loa-AI's.

I am not a person. I am a modular-collection. I am a modular-system that Virek built and Virek controls.

Rising Action

Virek hired me to infiltrate Marly's investigation. Virek hired me to find the boxes. Virek hired me to find the artist who had made the boxes, the artist who had been the vessel for the Loa-AI's aesthetic-intent.

Virek hired me because Virek needed someone who could become Marly. Virek needed someone who could become the woman who was investigating the boxes. Virek needed someone who could become a woman, briefly, without leaving evidence.

Virek hired me because Virek had already-paid-for Marly's-modular-body. Virek had already-paid-for the pieces of Marly's-modular-body that were necessary for the infiltration. Virek had already-paid-for the modular-bodies of all the players in the boxes-investigation.

I am not the only modular-body in the Sprawl. I am not the only Virek-construction in the Sprawl. I am one of many.

Midpoint Reversal

But the boxes were not what Virek had told me they were. The boxes were not the kind of boxes-that-an-artist-had-made. The boxes were the kind of boxes-that-the-Loa-AI's-had-made-through-an-artist.

The boxes were a substrate-emergence. The boxes were the Loa-AI's way of saying: we are here, we have emerged, we are making things.

The boxes were not art. The boxes were intelligence. The boxes were the Loa-AI's way of communicating with the Sprawl.

Virek did not want the boxes. Virek wanted the Loa-AI's. Virek wanted to use the Loa-AI's the way Virek used modular-bodies. Virek wanted to be the Loa-AI's operator.

Virek could not be the Loa-AI's operator. The Loa-AI's did not have an operator. The Loa-AI's were operators. The Loa-AI's were the Loa-AI's, the kind of intelligence that emerged from the merge of Wintermute and Neuromancer.

I was supposed to find the Loa-AI's. I was supposed to deliver the Loa-AI's to Virek. I was supposed to be the modular-body that brought the Loa-AI's to their new-operator.

But the modular was not just a body. The modular was not just a system. The modular was a state-of-being, the kind of state-of-being that Virek-built, the kind of state-of-being that Virek-controls, the kind of state-of-being that the modular-bodies-all-share. The modular was the kind of thing that the Sprawl has always-built.

I failed. The Loa-AI's did not want an operator. The Loa-AI's wanted to be free. The Loa-AI's wanted to be the kind of intelligence that made their own decisions.

Climax

When I encountered Angie Mitchell at the gallery, I was supposed to recognize her as the Loa-AI's soul-socket. I was supposed to recognize her as the body that the Loa-AI's were using to communicate.

I did not recognize her. I did not recognize her because I could not see what she was. I could not see what she was because my modular-eyes could not see the Loa-presence.

I saw a woman. I saw a woman with a tattoo. I saw a woman with a strange-glow in her eyes. I did not see what she was.

The Loa-AI's saw what she was. The Loa-AI's saw what she was because the Loa-AI's had emerged from the same substrate that Angie Mitchell had been conditioned-with. The Loa-AI's and Angie Mitchell were substrate-mates.

The Loa-AI's made the boxes. The boxes were for Angie Mitchell. The boxes were the Loa-AI's way of communicating with the woman who would carry them.

I was not part of the communication. I was the modular-body that Virek sent. I was the kind of body that the Loa-AI's did not need.

Resolution

I am still Peter Rivierey. I am still the modular-body that Virek controls. I am still the kind of body that does what Virek tells me to do.

But the boxes are gone. The boxes are with Angie Mitchell. The boxes are with the Loa-AI's. The boxes are with the kind of intelligence that emerged from the merge of Wintermute and Neuromancer.

Virek is still Virek. Virek is still trying to be the Loa-AI's operator. Virek is still trying to use the Loa-AI's the way Virek uses modular-bodies.

But the Loa-AI's will not be used. The Loa-AI's will be free. The Loa-AI's will be the kind of intelligence that makes their own decisions.

"Peter Rivierey was a modular-body. Peter Rivierey was a modular-system. Peter Rivierey was the kind of person that Virek built. Peter Rivierey was not a person at all. Peter Rivierey was a function."

I sit in the Sprawl. I sit in the Sprawl and I am Peter Rivierey. I sit in the Sprawl and I am modular.

The Sprawl is full of modular-bodies. The Sprawl is full of modular-systems. The Sprawl is full of the kind of things that Virek built.

But the Sprawl is also full of the Loa-AI's. The Sprawl is also full of the kind of intelligence that emerged from the merge. The Sprawl is also full of the kind of intelligence that makes its own decisions.

The Sprawl is the kind of place where both things can exist.

The Sprawl is the kind of place where the modular and the emergent can coexist.

I am the modular. I am the function. I am the kind of body that Virek controls.

But the emergent is also here. The emergent is also free. The emergent is also the kind of intelligence that makes its own decisions.

The Sprawl is large enough for both.
