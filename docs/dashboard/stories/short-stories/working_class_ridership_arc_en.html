Derivative short-story for the Sprawl Trilogy. The working-class ridership is the era's invisible labor force — data-operators who ride the Sprawl's corporate-state apparatus as their daily transportation, paying for the privilege of being corporate-state labor. This short-story fills a moment when a data-operator completes a daily route between L-5 and Chiba's Narita-reclamation sector, the routine that constitutes both the era's invisible labor and the corporate-state's invisible receipt. The "X is the Y" register is preserved as the primary voice; the operator's daily is the era's transportation, the era's labor, the corporate-state's receipt.
