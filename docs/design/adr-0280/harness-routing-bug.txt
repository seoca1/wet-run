ADR-0280 — a measurement bug that invalidated the first curve tuning.

WHAT WAS WRONG
The run policy (`scripts/play_policy.ts`) navigated to the BOSS with a BFS
shortest path. `first_jack` is:

  node 0 (start, no ICE)  adj [1, 2]
  node 1 (wisp)           adj [0, 3]
  node 2 (no ICE)         adj [0, 4, 5]
  node 5 (boss)           adj [2, 3]

so 0 -> 2 -> 5 reaches the boss with NO encounter at all. The policy took that
path, which means every measurement was a BOSS-ONLY run: no normal fight, no
non-terminal loot screen, therefore NO CARD REWARD and no deck growth. Measured
deck size at the end was 5.0-5.2 cards and best tier 3 at every grade, with 7.4
encounters at g5 -- the build-up the run is built around never happened.

The policy now walks to the NEAREST unresolved encounter, which is what playing
the mission means.

WHAT IT INVALIDATED
The curve committed in the previous step was tuned against the boss-only metric,
so its "grades 1-4 hit their targets" claim does not hold. Re-measured honestly
(20 seeds x 53 missions, opening deck):

                      g1     g2     g3     g4     g5     g6
  boss-only (wrong)  100.0  99.1   33.2   11.5    0.0    0.0
  honest             100.0  96.6   71.8   50.0    0.0    0.0
  target             100    95     80     50     25     10

The honest baseline is materially harder at g3/g4 because the runner now fights
the encounters the mission actually contains.

WHAT SURVIVED
- The boss-per-grade fix (ADR-0280 §5) is unaffected: it is about which ICE
  spawns, not about routing.
- The per-encounter curve (`balance_sim`) is unaffected: it never navigates.
- The finding that the deck does NOT grow is now stronger, not weaker: at g4-g6
  the runner dies in its FIRST fight (0.6-0.8 fights/run), so it never reaches a
  second reward. Cards cannot rescue a run that loses the opening fight.

FURTHER CONFIRMATION OF THE g5/g6 WALL
Tested whether a stronger STARTING deck is the lever, using the grade-appropriate
deck `balance_sim.deckForGrade` (every program of tier <= grade, strongest five):

  starting deck   g1     g2     g3     g4     g5     g6
  opening        100.0  75.8   51.7   20.6    0.0    0.0
  ppl            100.0  68.4   64.8   28.7    0.7    2.1

Even the developed deck leaves g5 at 0.7%. The per-encounter win rate at g5 is
~41% (harness), and a g5 mission is 7.4 encounters, so survival is ~0 no matter
what the deck is. Deck strength is not the binding constraint either.
