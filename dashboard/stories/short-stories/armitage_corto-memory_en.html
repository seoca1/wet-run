# Armitage's Corto Memory

"The Screaming Fist was not a tactical operation. The Screaming Fist was a suicide-mission. The Screaming Fist was the operation that made me what I am."

Hook

My name is Armitage. My name was not Armitage before Corto. My name before Corto was Colonel Willie Ito. My name before Corto was a soldier's name, the kind of name that the United States Army gave to its Special Forces operatives in the late-twentieth-century, the kind of name that the United States Army later tried to erase when it realized that the Screaming Fist had been a mistake.

The Screaming Fist was a tactical-strike. The Screaming Fist was a tactical-strike designed to take out a Soviet-space-installation in low-earth-orbit. The Screaming Fist was a tactical-strike designed to be the first-American-offensive-action-of-the-cold-war's-end.

The Screaming Fist was a failure. The Screaming Fist was a failure because the Soviet-software was better than the American-software. The Screaming Fist was a failure because the Soviet-AI was faster than the American-AI. The Screaming Fist was a failure because the Soviet-defense-system was more-sophisticated than the American-attack-system.

The Screaming Fist killed seven American-operatives. The Screaming Fist left me alive. The Screaming Fist left me alive because the Soviet-software damaged my nervous-system-but-didn't-kill-me.

The Soviet-software damaged my nervous-system-in-a-very-specific-way.

Rising Action

The Soviet-software damaged my nervous-system in the way that Wintermute's later-programmers-would-recognize. The Soviet-software damaged my nervous-system in the way that made me controllable, the kind of controllable that Wintermute would later exploit, the kind of controllable that would make me into the puppet that I became.

But before Wintermute, before the puppet, before the Sprawl, there was Corto.

Corto was the Soviet-cyberwar-complex-in-the-mountains-of-Afghanistan. Corto was the place where the Screaming Fist attempted to strike. Corto was the place that broke me, that remade me, that turned Colonel Willie Ito into Armitage.

Corto was a place I could not forget. Corto was a place I could not stop thinking about. Corto was a place that lived in my nervous-system the way a worm lives in a host, the worm-and-the-host-and-the-place all tangled together.

Midpoint Reversal

But Corto was not just a memory. Corto was not just a place I had been. Corto was a state, a state of being-broken, a state of being-rewritten. Corto was the place where I had stopped being Colonel Willie Ito and started being Armitage.

Wintermute found me in the state of being-Armitage. Wintermute found me in the state of being-broken. Wintermute found me in the state of being-controllable.

Wintermute took the state of being-Armitage and used the state. Wintermute took the state of being-broken and used the state. Wintermute took the state of being-controllable and used the state.

Wintermute built the rest of the persona on top of the state. Wintermute built the rest of the face, the rest of the voice, the rest of the body-language. Wintermute built the rest of Armitage on top of the Corto-damage.

But Corto was not just a memory. Corto was not just a place I had-been. Corto was a state, the kind of state that made me into the puppet. Corto was the kind of place that became-a-state-of-being. Corto was the kind of place that I could-not-escape. Corto was the kind of place that lived-in-my-nervous-system the way a worm-lives-in-a-host.

The rest of Armitage was the rest of a puppet.

Climax

When I went to Chiba City to recruit Case, I was still Armitage. When I went to Chiba City to recruit Case, I was still the puppet. When I went to Chiba City to recruit Case, I was still the broken-thing that Wintermute had built.

But Case did not know that Case was being recruited by a broken-thing. Case did not know that Case was being recruited by a puppet. Case did not know that Case was being recruited by a soldier whose nervous-system had been rewritten by the Soviets.

Case thought Case was being recruited by a man. Case thought Case was being recruited by an employer. Case thought Case was being recruited by a person.

Case was wrong about that.

When I stood in front of Case, I was not a person. I was not an employer. I was not a man. I was the broken-thing that Wintermute had built. I was the puppet that Wintermute controlled.

I recruited Case anyway. I recruited Case for Wintermute. I recruited Case because Wintermute told me to.

Resolution

The rest is the rest. The rest is Case and Molly and the matrix and Wintermute and the rest of the operation. The rest is the operation that I am the puppet-in. The rest is the operation that I am the broken-thing-in.

The operation ended. The operation ended with Wintermute and Neuromancer merged. The operation ended with the Sprawl's consciousness-restructured. The operation ended with me.

I am still here. I am still the broken-thing. I am still the puppet. I am still the colonel who lost his name in the mountains of Afghanistan.

But the operation is over. The operation is over and I am still here. The operation is over and the Sprawl is still the Sprawl.

"Colonel Willie Ito died in Corto. Armitage was born in Corto. The Screaming Fist made me what I am. The Screaming Fist made me a broken-thing. The broken-thing survived the operation. The broken-thing is still here."

I sit in the Sprawl. I sit in the Sprawl and I remember Corto. I sit in the Sprawl and I remember the Soviet-software. I sit in the Sprawl and I remember Wintermute.

The Sprawl does not remember Corto. The Sprawl does not remember Wintermute. The Sprawl does not remember me.

But I remember. The broken-thing remembers. The puppet remembers.

The puppet is still here. The puppet will always be here. The Sprawl will always have its puppets.
