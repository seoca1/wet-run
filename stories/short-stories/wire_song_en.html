I don't know if that's true. I don't know what counts as a strand. I don't know if a strand is one wire or one bundle or one rope. I don't know if the wire is the same as the cable. I don't know if the cable is the same as the bridge.

But I do know the song.

The song is the sound that the wires make when the wind is right. The wind is right when it's coming off the Pacific and not too hard and not too soft. The wind is right when it makes the bridge hum. The bridge hums when the wires sing.
