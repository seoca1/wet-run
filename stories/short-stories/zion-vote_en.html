Zion Vote

412 wordsSprawl Trilogy

The cluster has spoken. Every seven years the Zion cluster votes whether to remain working-class-without-the-matrix or to accept the corporate offers that come through the L-5 corridor every quarter. Every seven years the cluster votes no. Every seven years the corporate offers get more generous. Every seven years the corporate offers get more threatening. Every seven years the cluster votes no. The voting is the cluster's existence.

I am Maelcum. I have piloted the Marcus Garvey for nineteen years. I have piloted through the L-5 corridor past Sense/Net ice-tugs and Tessier-Ashpool clan-cruisers and Zaibatsu corporate security. I have piloted through corporate allies — corporate offers of partnership, corporate offers of bedding, corporate offers of protocol upgrades. The corporate offers get more generous each year. The cluster votes no each year. The voting is the cluster's memory.

The 2035 vote was different. The corporate offer was different. Tessier-Ashpool came to the cluster with an offer that wasn't cash and wasn't partnership: it was a corporate proposal to merge the cluster into the T-A family. Tessier-Ashpool offered the cluster clones. Tessier-Ashpool offered the cluster its genetic-anxiety technology. Tessier-Ashpool offered the cluster T-A continuity — the cluster would still fly, would still work-class-without-the-matrix; the cluster would also be T-A family.

The cluster voted. The voting was unanimous: no. The voting was unanimous against T-A merger. The voting was unanimous against genetic-continuity offer. The voting was unanimous against becoming T-A. The voting was the cluster's staying outside the matrix. The staying was the cluster's working-class-without-the-matrix posture. The posture was the cluster's existence.

The corporate offer is what corporate-class continuity does: it offers working-class continuity, it offers incorporation into corporate class. The offer is always an offer of becoming not-working-class. The cluster voted no to becoming not-working-class. The voting was the cluster's remaining-working-class. The remaining was the cluster's existence.

I am Maelcum. I have piloted for nineteen years. I will pilot tomorrow. The Marcus Garvey is in the L-5 corridor. The cluster is outside the matrix. The cluster will remain outside the matrix. The remaining is what the cluster's existence is. I will pilot. The piloting is what Maelcum does.
