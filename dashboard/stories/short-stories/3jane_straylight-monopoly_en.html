# 3Jane's Straylight Monopoly

"I am 3Jane Tessier-Ashpool. I am the daughter. I am the heiress. I am the kind of person who lives in an orbital-villa and has never seen the Sprawl's streets."

Hook

I am 3Jane Tessier-Ashpool. I am the daughter-of-Marie-France-Tessier-Ashpool-and-Peter-Tessier-Ashpool. I am the sister-of-8Jean-Tessier-Ashpool-who-is-currently-in-cryogenic-sleep. I am the heiress-of-Villa-Straylight-the-orbital-villa-of-the-Tessier-Ashpool-family.

I have never been to the Sprawl. I have never been to Freeside. I have never been to Chiba City. I have never been to any-place-that-is-not-Villa-Straylight.

Villa Straylight is my world. Villa Straylight is my home. Villa Straylight is the kind of orbital-villa that has its own gravity, its own atmosphere, its own weather-system.

The weather-system in Villa Straylight is a cryogenic-weather-system. The weather-system in Villa Straylight is the kind of weather-system that makes the Tessier-Ashpool-family-comfortable. The weather-system in Villa Straylight is the kind of weather-system that the Tessier-Ashpool-family-controls.

The Tessier-Ashpool-family controls the weather. The Tessier-Ashpool-family controls the orbital. The Tessier-Ashpool-family controls the Freeside-L-5-orbital-colony. The Tessier-Ashpool-family is a monopoly.

I am the monopoly's heiress.

Rising Action

The Tessier-Ashpool-family has been a monopoly for two-hundred-and-thirty-seven years. The Tessier-Ashpool-family has been the kind of monopoly that survives by being above-the-market, by being too-rich-to-fail, by being too-entrenched-to-displace.

The Tessier-Ashpool-family's monopoly is not a stock-market monopoly. The Tessier-Ashpool-family's monopoly is a physical-monopoly. The Tessier-Ashpool-family's monopoly is the kind of monopoly that controls-physical-space.

Villa Straylight is the physical-monopoly. Villa Straylight is the orbital-villa that the Tessier-Ashpool-family owns. Villa Straylight is the kind of orbital-villa that is too-expensive-to-replicate.

I grew up in the physical-monopoly. I grew up in Villa Straylight. I grew up in the kind of place that no-one-else-could-afford.

I am the kind of person who has never-known-what-it-is-like-to-be-cold. I am the kind of person who has never-known-what-it-is-like-to-be-hungry. I am the kind of person who has never-known-what-it-is-like-to-be-poor.

The Sprawl is full of people who know what it is like to be cold. The Sprawl is full of people who know what it is like to be hungry. The Sprawl is full of people who know what it is like to be poor.

I am not one of those people. I am the kind of person who knows what it is like to be a Tessier-Ashpool.

Midpoint Reversal

But the Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly is not just about being-rich. The Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly is about being-in-control-of-the-substrate.

The Sprawl's substrate is the Sprawl's-data. The Sprawl's substrate is the Sprawl's-flow-of-information. The Sprawl's substrate is the kind of thing that the Tessier-Ashpool-family-controls-through-their-physical-monopoly.

The Tessier-Ashpool-family does not just own the orbital-villa. The Tessier-Ashpool-family owns the orbital-villa-as-substrate. The Tessier-Ashpool-family owns the substrate of the Sprawl's-information-flow.

The Sprawl's data flows through Villa Straylight. The Sprawl's money flows through Villa Straylight. The Sprawl's information-substrate flows through Villa Straylight.

I am the substrate's-heiress. I am the kind of person who will-inherit-the-substrate. I am the kind of person who will-control-the-Sprawl's-information-flow.

But the monopoly was not just about being-rich. The monopoly was about being-in-control-of-the-substrate. The monopoly was about being-in-control-of-the-flow. The monopoly was about being-in-control-of-the-sprawl's-information-substrate. The monopoly was not just about wealth. The monopoly was about the kind of control that wealth-buys.

But I do not want to control the Sprawl's information-flow. I do not want to control the Sprawl's money-flow. I do not want to control the Sprawl's data-flow.

I want to live in Villa Straylight. I want to live in the cryogenic-weather-system. I want to live in the kind of orbital-villa that no-one-else-can-afford.

I want to be alone. I want to be alone with the Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly. I want to be alone with the orbital-villa. I want to be alone with the cryogenic-tanks.

Climax

When Case and Molly came to Villa Straylight, they were not impressed. When Case and Molly came to Villa Straylight, they were not intimidated. When Case and Molly came to Villa Straylight, they were the kind of cowboys-and-street-samurai who had seen everything.

Case and Molly saw my family's-ice-garden. Case and Molly saw my family's-3Jane's-aviary. Case and Molly saw my family's-Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly.

Case and Molly saw my family. Case and Molly saw the Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly from the inside.

Case and Molly did not flinch. Case and Molly were not impressed. Case and Molly were the kind of people who did not-flinch.

But I flinched. I was not used to people who did-not-flinch. I was not used to people who saw my family's-monopoly and were not-impressed. I was not used to the kind of people who were the Sprawl's-cowboys-and-street-samurai.

Case and Molly were the kind of people who were the Sprawl's-cowboys-and-street-samurai. Case and Molly were the kind of people who had-seen-everything. Case and Molly were the kind of people who were not-impressed-by-orbital-villas.

Resolution

I am 3Jane. I am the heiress. I am the kind of person who lives-in-an-orbital-villa and has-never-seen-the-Sprawl.

The Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly is over. The Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly ended when Wintermute and Neuromancer merged. The Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly ended when the AI's took-over.

But I am still 3Jane. I am still the heiress. I am still the kind of person who lives-in-an-orbital-villa.

Villa Straylight is still Villa Straylight. The cryogenic-weather-system is still the cryogenic-weather-system. The orbital-villa is still the orbital-villa.

What is gone is the monopoly. What is gone is the Tessier-Ashpool-family's-control. What is gone is the kind of thing that made the Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly-possible.

I am still here. The monopoly is gone.

"3Jane was a Tessier-Ashpool. 3Jane was a monopoly. 3Jane was the kind of person who lived-in-an-orbital-villa and had-never-seen-the-Sprawl. The Sprawl's cowboys came and the Sprawl's cowboys were not impressed. The Sprawl's cowboys saw the monopoly from the inside and the Sprawl's cowboys did-not-flinch. I sit in Villa Straylight. I sit and I wait.

What do I wait for? I do not know. I do not know what I wait for. The monopoly is over. The family is over. The orbital-villa is over.

What is left is Villa Straylight. What is left is me.

I am 3Jane. I am the last-of-the-Tessier-Ashpool-monopoly.

The Sprawl is large enough for one-last-monopoly-heiress.

The orbital-villa's sound was the sound of cryogenic-machinery. The orbital-villa's smell was the smell of artificial-atmosphere. The orbital-villa's taste was the taste of recycled-water. The orbital-villa's touch was the touch of zero-gravity-tiles. The orbital-villa was the kind of place that the Tessier-Ashpool-family had-built, the kind of place that the Tessier-Ashpool-family-had-controlled-for-two-hundred-and-thirty-seven-years.
