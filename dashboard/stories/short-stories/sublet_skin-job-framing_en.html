Sublet: Skin-Job Framing

967 wordsBridge Trilogy

"Sublet had been a skin-job for nine years. The job was the only identity she'd kept that long."

Hook

Sublet stood in front of the mirror in her third-floor-walkup and tried to remember what her face had looked like before the framing. The framing was the skin-job, the layer of polymer-and-pigment that had replaced the face she had been born with. The skin-job had cost her eight months of work and twelve thousand dollars of somebody-else's money. The skin-job had given her a different bone-structure, a different eye-color, a different everything.

The skin-job had also given her the framing. The framing was the job. The framing was the way the skin-job made people look at her without seeing her. The framing was the distance between the person she had been and the person she was now.

Rising Action

Sublet's clients were mostly corporate. The corporate-clients wanted to be seen with someone who was beautiful-but-not-too-beautiful, someone who was present-but-not-present. The corporate-clients wanted a person who could sit at a dinner-party and not be noticed, except by the corporate-clients themselves, who noticed the skin-job as if it were a piece of furniture they had bought.

The corporate-clients paid well. The corporate-clients paid for Sublet's time, for Sublet's framing, for Sublet's silence. The corporate-clients paid for the privilege of being seen with someone whose face they did not have to look at.

Sublet did not mind the framing. Sublet had learned to live with the framing. Sublet had learned to live through the framing, the way she had once lived through her original-face, before the original-face had been a liability.

The original-face had been a liability because it had been her father's face. Her father had been a corporate-fixer's associate. Her father had been killed in a corporate-deal that had gone wrong. Sublet's original-face had been the face that had been there when the corporate-deal had gone wrong. The corporate-clients who had killed her father had seen Sublet's original-face, and they had remembered it, and they had not hired Sublet for any framing-job.

Midpoint Reversal

But the framing was not what the corporate-clients wanted anymore. The corporate-clients wanted something else. The corporate-clients wanted someone whose skin-job was not a skin-job at all, but a kind of literal-extension of the body, a kind of cybernetic-overlay that could be removed and reinstalled and removed again, a kind of skin-job-as-tool. The corporate-clients had moved on. The corporate-clients had stopped wanting framing.

They wanted a different Sublet. They wanted a Sublet who was not a person, but a kind of cybernetic-apparatus. They wanted a Sublet who could be removed and reinstalled. They wanted a Sublet who could be controlled.

Climax

Sublet looked at herself in the mirror. The skin-job was good. The skin-job was nine years old. The skin-job had held up. The skin-job had been worth the twelve thousand dollars.

But the skin-job was a frame. The skin-job was a frame around a face that the corporate-clients had never seen. The skin-job was a frame around a body that the corporate-clients had touched without feeling. The skin-job was a frame around a person who had learned to live behind the frame.

The corporate-clients were not going to hire her anymore. The corporate-clients were going to hire someone with cybernetic-overlay. The corporate-clients were going to hire someone whose skin-job was not a skin-job but a cybernetic-apparatus.

Sublet sat on the bed. The bed smelled like her. The bed smelled like the nine years she had spent in this walkup. The bed smelled like the framing and the silence and the corporate-clients and the absence of her father.

Resolution

She took off the skin-job. She took off the framing. She took off the polymer-and-pigment and the bone-structure-replacement and the eye-color-change. She took off the nine years of framing.

Underneath, her original-face was still there. Her original-face had aged. Her original-face had wrinkles now, and grey-hair at the temples, and a kind of tired-set to the mouth. Her original-face was not a corporate-fixer's-associate's-face anymore. Her original-face was just a face. Her original-face was just a person who had been through a lot.

She looked at herself in the mirror. The person looking back at her was not the corporate-clients' Sublet. The person looking back at her was Sublet's Sublet. The person looking back at her was the person she had been before the framing.

"The framing had been a job. The framing had also been a hiding. Sublet had been hiding for nine years. Now she was just standing in front of a mirror, in a walkup, on the bridge, looking at herself."

She put the skin-job back on. She put the framing back on. She put the polymer-and-pigment and the bone-structure-replacement and the eye-color-change back on. She put the corporate-clients' Sublet back on.

But she had seen the original-face. She had seen the person underneath. She had seen Sublet's Sublet.

That was the framing she would keep. That was the framing that would last longer than the polymer-and-pigment.
