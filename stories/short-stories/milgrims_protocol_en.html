Milgrim's Protocol

A Recovering Operative Counts the Hours Until the Next Dose

256 wordsBlue Ant Trilogy

The hotel was in Shibuya. The hotel had been, in any record Milgrim had been given, the hotel of a kind that did not ask questions of foreign guests.

Milgrim sat on the bed. The protocol was the protocol of the Fitch clinic. The protocol had been administered since 2003.

The protocol did not allow him to refuse. The refusing had been the refusing of a body that had been, since 2003, the body of a man who could not say no.

Cayce was in the room next door. Cayce had been in the room next door since Tuesday.

The phone rang.

"Milgrim."

"Yes."

"The walk is at nine. You will wear the jacket. You will not speak to anyone."

"Yes."

The phone hung up. Milgrim counted the hours. The hours had been the hours of a body whose protocol was running low. The counting had been, in his private count, the only thing the protocol had not taken.

He looked at the jacket. The jacket was hung on the back of the door.

The jacket was black. The jacket had been the jacket of a kind that did not show brand. Cayce had chosen the brand. Cayce had chosen no brand.

In the morning, the refresh would come. The refresh had been the refresh of a body whose protocol had not, in any record he had been given, been denied.

Milgrim did not sleep. The sleep had been the sleep of a body whose protocol was running low.

He waited for morning.
