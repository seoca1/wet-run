Wigan's Dossier

A Blue Ant Consultant Walks the Footage

383 wordsSprawl Trilogy

The office was on a side street. The office was above a tailor's shop. The tailor's shop had been in the same family for three generations.

Wigan worked at a desk. Wigan had a small screen. Wigan had a small screen that showed the footage.

The footage was two months old. The footage had been on every network and every aggregator and every site that aggregated networks. The footage had been on every screen that Cayce Pollitt had watched. The footage had also been, in Wigan's private notes, on every screen that Cayce had not watched.

Wigan was looking for the gaps.

The gaps were where the footage was not. The gaps were where the footage should have been and was not. The gaps were the parts that someone had cut out.

He had been looking for the gaps for two months.

A knock at the door.

"Come in."

Bigend. Bigend never knocked. Bigend always entered. Bigend had entered and Bigend was now sitting in the chair across the desk.

"Wigan."

"Sir."

"How is the project?"

"The project is moving."

Bigend did not ask what the project was. Bigend already knew what the project was. Bigend had hired Wigan to find what Wigan had been hired to find.

"How is the project moving?"

"The project is moving toward the gap."

Bigend looked at him. Bigend was smiling. Bigend was smiling in a way that Bigend smiled when Bigend was about to say something that he had been waiting to say.

"There is no gap."

"No?"

"There is no gap. The footage is the footage. The footage is also the footage's negative. The negative is what we are tracking. The negative has been the negative for as long as the footage has been the footage."

Wigan did not understand.

"We hired you," Bigend said, "to confirm that there is no gap."

Wigan set down his pen.

"Sir."

"Yes."

"What was the footage, before the footage was the footage."

Bigend stood up.

"The footage was the footage," Bigend said. "The footage has always been the footage."

Bigend left.

Wigan sat at the desk.

Wigan had been looking for the gap for two months. The gap was not there. The gap had never been there.

Wigan closed the screen.
