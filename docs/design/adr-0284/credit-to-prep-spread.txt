WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE SALVAGE SLOT -- the honest version
A curve-safe stockpile cannot make SALVAGE competitive: a card or a repair is
still worth more, so the slot stays near-trap. Credits can only matter if the
tables are re-tuned DOWN to leave them headroom, which is a decision about
whether a returning runner should have an easier time than a new one.

Shipped: the curve-safe version, so credits are not a dead end and the tuned
curve is untouched. The re-tune is the open question.
