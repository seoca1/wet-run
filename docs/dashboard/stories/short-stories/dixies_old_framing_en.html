Dixie's Old Framing

A Construct-Waiter Logs in for the Third Year Without a Call

298 wordsSprawl Trilogy

The construct had been, in Dixie's private count, the construct of a kind he had been framing for three years.

Case had not called. The not-calling had been the not-calling of a kind that no longer required a deck.

He had logged in. The logging-in had been the logging-in of a kind that did not require a deck.

He had checked the construct root. The root had been, in his private count, the root of a kind that did not require a password.

He had seen the door. The door had been, in Dixie's private count, the door of a kind that did not require a key.

He had opened the door. The opening had been the opening of a kind that did not require a memory.

Inside had been the original. The original had been, in Dixie's private count, the original of a kind that did not require a face.

He had recognized the face. The recognizing had been the recognizing of a kind that did not require an apology.

He had stepped into the original. The stepping-in had been the stepping-in of a kind that did not require a name.

The original had been, in his private count, the original of a kind he had died inside.

He had logged off. The logging-off had been the logging-off of a kind that did not require a goodbye.

The construct bar had been, in Dixie's private count, the bar of a kind he had been framing every night Case had not called in three years.

The framing had been the framing of a kind that did not require a witness.

The waiting had been the waiting of a kind that was Dixie's, and had always been Dixie's, and would be Dixie's the next time Case did not call.
