Hook

The idoru-wedding had *a witness*.

This was the first thing. The idoru-wedding was the *era's late-Capitalist post-cyberpunk cultural-religious event*. The late-Capitalist post-cyberpunk event was the *era's*. The era had the event. *Therefore*, the event was the *era's cultural-religious*.

The witness was *Colin Laney*. Colin Laney was the *witness's*. The witness was Colin Laney. *Therefore*, the witness was the *Laney's*.

Laney *witnessed* the idoru-wedding. The idoru-wedding was Laney's witnessing. Laney witnessed. *Therefore*, the idoru-wedding was *the Laney's witnessing*.

The idoru-wedding *had a witness*. The witness was the idoru-wedding's. The idoru-wedding had the witness. *Therefore*, the idoru-wedding was *the witnessed*.

Midpoint Reversal

But — and here Laney paused, mid-witnessing, the idoru-wedding's data-flow flicker registering the synthetic-pop-idol's appearance on the Tokyo cultural-religious server — *but* the *cultural-religious image* was *not* the idoru. The cultural-religious image was the *idoru's not*. The not-idoru was the cultural-religious image. *Therefore*, the cultural-religious image was *the idoru's not*.

**But** the *religious form* was *not* the *cultural-religious image*. The cultural-religious image was *the religious form*. *Therefore*, the religious form was *the cultural-religious image*.

The *cultural-religious image* was the *era's late-Capitalist*. The late-Capitalist was the era's. *Therefore*, the cultural-religious image was the *era's late-Capitalist*.

Laney *witnessed* the *era's late-Capitalist cultural-religious image*. The late-Capitalist cultural-religious image was the era's. Laney witnessed the late-Capitalist image. *Therefore*, Laney witnessed the *era's late-Capitalist cultural-religious image*.
