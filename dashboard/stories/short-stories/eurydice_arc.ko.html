*Derivative short-story for the Sprawl Trilogy. The heretic is the operator archetype of Neuromancer — the underground worker who descends into corporate systems, audits the buried retainer-class profiles, and emerges with data the era needs to be reminded of. "Eurydice, Audited" fills the moment when a heretic descends into a layer-2 audit extract and brings out a small file — a profile of a buried operative-class retainer whose disappearance has been reclassified as "operational normalcy." The Orpheus-pattern is inverted: the heretic does not look back; the heretic emerges.*
