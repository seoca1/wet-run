Corto's Briefing

A Special Forces Commander Reviews the Mission Before He Leaves for Chiba

277 wordsSprawl Trilogy

The briefing had been finished. Corto had not yet left the room.

The room was in a compound outside Saigon. The compound had been the compound of a kind that did not have a name outside the building.

The briefing had been about a man named Ashpool. Ashpool had been the man in orbit. The orbit had been the orbit of a kind that did not require Corto's presence.

Corto had signed the form. The form had been the form of a kind that signed away his name for the duration of the mission.

The mission was to be flown out at dawn. The dawn had been the dawn of a kind that did not wait for the man to be ready.

He walked to the window. The window had been the window of a kind that did not open.

Outside the compound a child had been crying. The crying had been the crying of a kind that did not, in any record he had been given, require a father.

Corto had been a father. The father had been the father of a kind that did not see his daughter again.

The mission had been about an orbital heist. The heist had been the heist of a kind that would take Corto's face and replace it.

The man in the mirror had been the man Corto had been for thirty-eight years. The man in the mirror had not been the man who would arrive in Chiba.

Corto put on his cap.

He went to the door. The door had been the door of a kind that closed behind him.

He did not look back.
