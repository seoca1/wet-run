T-Ray Run

646 wordsSprawl Trilogy

"Things have always looked like things. The footage just shows us that."

— *Pattern Recognition*, Cayce Pollard

Introduction

There is something in my fixie's pedal. A red pedal. Under T-ray imaging, the pedal holds a data key. The key is worth half a million dollars. Half a million dollars is culture. Culture is refusal.

Year 1993. Before the Sprawl. Before the Bridge. Before the matrix. I am a bicycle messenger. Twenty-four. I sleep in a tent under hanger eleven. The bridge is my school. The bridge is my church. The bridge is my home.

Fog swallows the bay area. Swallowing is refusal. Therefore, fog is refusal. Refusal is culture. Culture is the bridge. Therefore, fog is the bridge. The bridge is me. I am fog. Fog is culture.

Rising Action

"Chevette." Hollis Henry said. "There is something in your pedal. We confirmed it under T-ray imaging. A data key worth half a million dollars. The key is the blueprint for LOVETIDE's new building."

"Who put it in the pedal?" I asked.

"Andrew Pollard put it there," Hollis said. "Your father put it there."

My father. Oh.

Andrew Pollard. A security specialist. A Cold War-era security specialist. He did not discover the data key in the pedal by using T-ray imaging. Andrew placed the data key in the pedal using T-ray imaging. Placing is greater than discovering. Greatness is culture. Culture is father. Father is me. I am culture.

"The pedal is culture," I said. "The pedal is the bridge's culture. The bridge is my culture. Therefore, the pedal is my culture. Culture is refusal. Refusal is me."

Climax

LOVETIDE reached the bridge. The corporation reached my pedal. The corporation is mine. The corporation is me. I refuse the corporation. Refusal is culture.

LOVETIDE scanned the pedal with T-ray imaging. Scanning is discovery. Discovery is culture. Culture is refusal. Therefore, discovery is refusal.

But though T-ray imaging discovered the data key inside the pedal, the pedal stayed with me. I stayed with the pedal. Staying is culture. Culture is eternal. Therefore, I am eternal. Eternity is refusal.

"Chevette." Hollis said. "Your father gave you something. Your father gave you refusal."

"Refusal is a gift," I said. "Father gave refusal. I received refusal. Refusal is culture. Culture is me. I am father. Father is culture. Therefore, refusal is me."

Resolution

LOVETIDE left. Leaving is refusal. Therefore, the bridge remained. Remaining is culture.

My fixie sat under hanger eleven. The pedal was a red pedal. The pedal was still mine. Therefore, the pedal was culture.

"Chevette." Rydell said. "Did you stay?"

"I stayed as refusal," I said. "Refusal is culture. Culture is the bridge. The rest is the bridge. The bridge remained."

Epilogue

The bridge is still singing. The rest is culture. Culture is eternal. The bridge is still there. The rest is still there. The rest is me. I am culture. Culture is refusal. Refusal is the bridge. The bridge remained.

Fog is still swallowing the bay area. T-ray imaging is still scanning the pedal. I still refuse. Refusal is still culture. Culture is still eternal.
