## Overview

**Molly Millions** is one of the Sprawl's most recognizable body-modified combat specialists — a street samurai whose surgically enhanced reflexes and retractable blade fingernails make her the perfect wetwork operative. She appears as a **combat ally / rival NPC** in Wet Run, drawing on the canonical character from William Gibson's *Neuromancer* (1984).
