Warbaby's Roster

An IntenSecure Chief Reviews the Tokyo Detail

259 wordsBridge Trilogy

The roster had been printed twice. Warbaby had read it twice.

He sat at the desk IntenSecure had given him in the Tokyo office. The office had been the office of a kind that did not, in any record he had been given, have windows.

The roster had four names. The four names had been the four names of the team going to the detail in Shibuya.

The first name had been Rydell. Rydell had been the man Warbaby had picked. The picking had been the picking of a man who had been fired from one job and rehired at another.

The second name had been a woman. The woman had been the woman who would mind the cameras. The cameras had been the cameras of a kind that watched all night.

The third name had been a translator. The translator had been the translator of a kind that had worked for IntenSecure since 2002.

The fourth name had been Warbaby. Warbaby had been the man who would not leave Tokyo until the detail was over.

The detail was a Lovelace client. Lovelace had been Lovelace since the year Warbaby had been hired.

He initialed the roster. The initialing had been the initialing of a man who had initialed thousands of rosters before.

He sent the roster to the office. The office had been the office of a kind that did not call back.

He poured a coffee. The coffee had been the coffee of a kind that did not taste like coffee.

He drank it.
