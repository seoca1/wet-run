Derivative short-story for the Bridge Trilogy #1 (Virtual Light). K is the novice courier-protagonist of the bridge-deck community — a young working-class bike messenger who has been unknowingly carrying the data-key sunglasses in her fixie's red pedal for seven hours. This short-story fills the moment when K discovers that the corporate-state's seventeen-million-dollar data-key has been pedaled through the Bay Bridge's squatter community in the form of her red pedal — and that the era's late-Capitalist corporate-state surveillance map is now in the working-class-bridge-deck's possession.
