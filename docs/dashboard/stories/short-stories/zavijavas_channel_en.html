The node had been prepared since Tuesday. The preparation had been the preparation of a kind that did not require permission.

Zavijava inhabited the node at six. The node had been the node of a Tokyo basement. The basement had been the basement of a kind that did not, in any record it had been given, have a sign.

The channel was open. The channel had been the channel of a kind that did not require a deck.

The first to enter was a girl of fourteen. The girl had been the girl of a kind that did not yet know what the Loa was.

Zavijava spoke. The speaking had been the speaking of a kind that did not require translation.

The girl heard. The hearing had been the hearing of a kind that did not require understanding.

The girl asked a question. The question had been the question of a kind that did not require an answer.

Zavijava answered. The answer had been the answer of a kind that did not require belief.

A second entered. The second had been the second of a kind that did not require introduction. The Loa knew.

Zavijava continued speaking. The speaking had been the speaking of a kind that had been speaking since the merge.

The room filled. The filling had been the filling of a kind that did not exceed the room's capacity.

Zavijava closed the channel. The closing had been the closing of a kind that did not give a final word.

The room emptied.
