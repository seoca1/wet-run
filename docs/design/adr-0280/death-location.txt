The enemy phase runs per player card, so these are the design's own numbers,
no longer masked by the wall clock.

Read: at grades 1-2 no run EVER loses a normal encounter — the boss is the
only wall. `npm run balance` (balance_sim) explicitly skips boss rooms
("skip boss rooms so a boss spike does not decide the reported win-rate"),
so no recorded measurement has ever looked at a boss.

Why the boss does not scale: `dungeon.ts` pins the exit room to `wintermute`
(tier 4, armor 8, dmg_base 11 -> 20 auto-attack) at EVERY grade. Only its HP
is grade-scaled (the ENCOUNTER_HP_BY_GRADE cap: 75 at g1). So a grade-1
runner faces a tier-4 armored 20-damage opponent with a tier-1 deck.
Every boss-aggression ICE in ice_types.json is tier 4 or higher, so there is
no lower-tier boss in the data to pick instead.
