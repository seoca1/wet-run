The logo had been finished on Thursday. Dorotea had been in the studio since Monday.

She stood at the head of the table. The table had been the table of a Tokyo hotel. The hotel had been the hotel of a kind that did not, in any record she had been given, take cash.

The presentation had been to Blue Ant. Blue Ant had been the client. The client had been the client of a kind that did not sign anything at the first meeting.

The logo was on the screen. The screen had been the screen of a kind that did not flicker.

Dorotea explained the colors. The colors had been the colors of a kind that did not, in any record she had been given, belong to any other brand.

She explained the geometry. The geometry had been the geometry of a kind she had been drawing since 1989.

She explained the type. The type had been the type of a kind she had set for the third time this year.

Across the table sat Cayce Pollard. Cayce had been the woman who would decide. The deciding had been the deciding of a woman whose father had once been in advertising.

Dorotea finished the slide. The slide had been the slide of a kind that did not show the secondary mark.

She asked for questions. The questions had been the questions of a kind that did not come until the slide had been on the screen for forty seconds.

Cayce sneezed.

Dorotea closed her laptop.
