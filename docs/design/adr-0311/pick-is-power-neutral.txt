ADR-0311 — is the pick power-neutral?

Question: with the instrument fixed (ADR-0310), does letting the player CHOOSE
the tier-6 unique raise g6 above the 30% target? If it does, the target has to
move with it.

Method: per-cell seeding, 64 seeds, optimal policy, shipped rule.

  g6 completion        ADR-0309 (first unique granted)   ADR-0311 (player picks)
  64 seeds                    32.3%  (62/192)                 32.3%  (62/192)

IDENTICAL COUNT. Which unique the runner gets does not move the mission outcome
in the harness. Two readings, both consistent with the design:

  1. The unique is one card in a growing deck. It is rarely dealt before the
     fight that decides the run, so the identity of the extra answer is worth
     less than the repair it sits beside (ADR-0305/0306).
  2. The three are sidegrades — same 30 damage, same cost — so the choice is
     about COVERAGE (which answer the deck lacks), not power.

So the pick restores agency at no measured cost, and the target still holds:

  grade    rate   target   delta
    1     100.0    100      +0.0
    2      97.1     95      +2.1
    3      75.9     80      -4.1
    4      51.4     50      +1.4
    5      26.9     25      +1.9
    6      32.3     30      +2.3

  worst miss 4.1pp (tolerance 8pp)

CAVEAT ON THE HARNESS
  The bot's cardScore ranks ghost_key (armor ignore) highest, so it picks the
  same answer in every run. A human picks for the deck in front of them, which
  the harness does not model — the measurement says the pick costs nothing, not
  that the choice is worthless.
