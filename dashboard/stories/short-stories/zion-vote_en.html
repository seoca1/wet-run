Derivative short-story for the Sprawl Trilogy. The Zion cluster is the orbital Rastafarian community that lives above the Sprawl in the L-5 corridor, in the microgravity where the matrix does not reach. Every seven years, the cluster votes whether to accept the corporate offers that come through the L-5 corridor. Every seven years for thirty-five years, the cluster has voted no. This short-story fills the moment of one such vote, the recounting by Maelcum, the council-member pilot, of why the no persists — because the cluster is not just voting against the offer, the cluster is the test itself.
