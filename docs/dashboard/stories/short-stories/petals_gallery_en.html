The back room had been cleared at noon. Petal had been clearing since ten.

The room was behind the gallery. The gallery had been the gallery of a kind that did not post a sign.

The room had a small table. The table had been the table of a kind that did not require a cloth.

On the table sat a small carved figure. The figure had been the figure of a kind that did not, in any record he had been given, have a written name.

Petal sat beside the table. The sitting had been the sitting of a desaideng. The desaideng had been the large-eye.

The figure buzzed. The buzzing had been the buzzing of a kind that did not require a battery.

The buzzing was the Loa. The Loa had been the Loa of a kind that did not require an invitation.

Petal watched. The watching had been the watching of a large-eye that did not look away.

The Loa spoke. The speaking had been the speaking of a kind that did not require translation.

The Loa said: a woman is coming.

Petal said nothing. The nothing had been the nothing of a desaideng who had heard this before.

A knock came at the door. The knock had been the knock of a kind that did not require a key.

Petal opened the door. The opening had been the opening of a door that did not lock from the inside.

Marly came in. The coming had been the coming of a kind Petal had been waiting for.

Petal poured tea.

Marly drank.
