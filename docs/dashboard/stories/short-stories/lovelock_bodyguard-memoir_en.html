Lovelock's Bodyguard Memoir

1031 wordsBridge Trilogy

"I have been WarBaby's bodyguard for nine years. I have been the bodyguard for the most-watched-person in the Sprawl. I have been the bodyguard for a person whose hands were worth more than the Sprawl's economy."

Hook

My name is Lovelock. My name was Lovelock before WarBaby. My name will be Lovelock after WarBaby. My name is the only thing about me that has not changed in nine years.

I am a bodyguard. I am the kind of bodyguard that the IntenSecure corporation hired for high-value-asset-protection. I am the kind of bodyguard that was hired for WarBaby.

WarBaby is not WarBaby's real-name. WarBaby is a nickname. WarBaby is the nickname that the Sprawl's fashion-press gave to a person whose hands were famous. WarBaby is the nickname that became more famous than WarBaby's real-name.

WarBaby's real-name is WarBaby's real-name. WarBaby's real-name is something that I know and that I do not say. WarBaby's real-name is the kind of thing that a bodyguard does-not-share.

But I am Lovelock. I am not WarBaby. I am the bodyguard, not the body. I am the kind of person whose real-name is the only thing that the Sprawl knows about me.

Rising Action

I have been WarBaby's bodyguard for nine years. I have been the bodyguard through nine years of corporate-events, fashion-shows, gallery-openings, and the kind of public-appearances that the Sprawl's famous-people make.

I have been the bodyguard through threats. I have been the bodyguard through the kind of threats that the Sprawl's-extremists make, the kind of threats that the Sprawl's-corporate-competitors make, the kind of threats that the Sprawl's-mentally-ill make.

I have been the bodyguard through attacks. I have been the bodyguard through three assassination-attempts. I have been the bodyguard through the kind of attacks that the Sprawl's-extremists-attempt.

I have never failed to protect WarBaby. I have never failed to be the bodyguard. I have never failed to be the kind of bodyguard that IntenSecure-hired.

But IntenSecure is not just a bodyguard-agency. IntenSecure is a corporate-entity. IntenSecure is the kind of corporate-entity that makes-decisions-based-on-money, the kind of corporate-entity that decides-which-bodyguards-to-keep and which-bodyguards-to-fire based-on-money.

IntenSecure decided to fire me. IntenSecure decided to fire me because IntenSecure could not afford me anymore. IntenSecure decided to fire me because WarBaby's contracts were decreasing, the kind of contracts that pay-for-bodyguards.

Midpoint Reversal

But the contracts did not decrease because of WarBaby. The contracts decreased because of the Sprawl. The contracts decreased because the Sprawl was changing, the kind of change that the Sprawl's-fashion-economy was experiencing, the kind of change that made WarBaby's-hands-less-valuable.

WarBaby's hands were valued because WarBaby's hands were the kind of hands that the Sprawl's-fashion-economy wanted. WarBaby's hands were the kind of hands that the Sprawl's-fashion-economy paid-to-see, the kind of hands that the Sprawl's-fashion-economy paid-to-photograph, the kind of hands that the Sprawl's-fashion-economy paid-to-touch.

But the Sprawl's-fashion-economy changed. The Sprawl's-fashion-economy stopped paying-to-see. The Sprawl's-fashion-economy stopped paying-to-photograph. The Sprawl's-fashion-economy stopped paying-to-touch.

The Sprawl's-fashion-economy started paying-to-virtually-see. The Sprawl's-fashion-economy started paying-to-virtually-photograph. The Sprawl's-fashion-economy started paying-to-virtually-touch.

WarBaby's real-hands became less-valuable. WarBaby's real-hands became the kind of hands that the Sprawl did-not-want-to-see.

Climax

When I was fired, I did not know what to do. When I was fired, I was Lovelock-without-a-WarBaby. When I was fired, I was the kind of person who did-not-know-what-to-do-with-himself.

I had been WarBaby's bodyguard for nine years. I had been the kind of person whose identity-was-the-job. I had been the kind of person who did-not-have-an-identity-beyond-the-job.

When the job was gone, I was gone. When the job was gone, I was the kind of person who was-nothing.

But the bridge-community took me in. The bridge-community took me in because the bridge-community was the kind of community that takes-in-people-who-have-nowhere-else-to-go. The bridge-community took me in because the bridge-community was the kind of community that does-not-ask-questions.

The bridge-community gave me a place. The bridge-community gave me a job. The bridge-community gave me the kind of place-and-job that the bridge-community-gives-to-people-who-have-nowhere-else-to-go.

The bridge-community gave me a place to be Lovelock-without-a-WarBaby. The bridge-community gave me a place to be Lovelock-the-bodyguard. The bridge-community gave me a place to be Lovelock-the-person.

Resolution

I am Lovelock. I am WarBaby's-former-bodyguard. I am the kind of person who lives-on-the-bridge now, the kind of person who works-for-the-bridge-community now.

I am not the kind of person who goes-back. I am not the kind of person who looks-back. I am the kind of person who lives-in-the-present.

WarBaby is still WarBaby. WarBaby is still the kind of person whose-hands-are-famous. WarBaby is still the kind of person who is-famous-for-hands.

But WarBaby is not Lovelock's-WarBaby anymore. WarBaby is WarBaby's-WarBaby. WarBaby is the kind of person who is-their-own-WarBaby.

I am Lovelock. I am the bridge-community's Lovelock. I am the kind of person who is-the-bridge-community's-bodyguard, the kind of person who guards-the-bridge-community's-body.

"Lovelock was a bodyguard. Lovelock was WarBaby's bodyguard. Lovelock was the kind of bodyguard that the IntenSecure corporation hired. Lovelock was fired. Lovelock went to the bridge. Lovelock became the bridge-community's bodyguard. Lovelock became the kind of person who guards-the-bridge."

The bridge is my home. The bridge-community is my family. The bridge's body is the kind of body that I guard.

I am Lovelock. I am the bridge's bodyguard. I am the kind of person who guards-the-bridge.

The Sprawl is large enough for one-more-bodyguard.
