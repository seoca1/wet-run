Colin: Nodal-Point Reading

1157 wordsBridge Trilogy

"The bridge had nodal-points. The nodal-points were the places where the bridge's attention-flow converged. The nodal-points were the places where the city could be seen."

Hook

I have been reading the bridge's nodal-points for seven years. I have been reading them with the Rooster, who taught me how to read them. I have been reading them with cameras that the corporate-attention-economy has stopped manufacturing. I have been reading them with the bridge-community, who tells me when my readings are true.

The nodal-points are the places on the bridge where the attention-flow converges. The nodal-points are the places where the corporate-attention-economy, the Lost-Norters, the Armenians, the Black Travelers, and the tourists all overlap. The nodal-points are the places where the bridge's many-substrate-economies intersect.

The nodal-points are not physical-locations. The nodal-points are not fixed-positions. The nodal-points are attention-events. The nodal-points are the moments when the bridge's many-attentions align.

Rising Action

The Rooster taught me how to find the nodal-points. The Rooster taught me that the nodal-points were the places where the bridge's attention-flow could be seen. The Rooster taught me that the nodal-points were the places where the city's underlying-substrate could be made visible.

The Rooster also taught me that the nodal-points were not always good. The Rooster taught me that the nodal-points were sometimes violent. The Rooster taught me that the nodal-points were sometimes sad. The Rooster taught me that the nodal-points were the places where the city's underlying-conditions surfaced.

I have been reading the nodal-points for seven years. I have made videos of the nodal-points. I have made videos that show what the nodal-points look like when the nodal-points are happening. I have made videos that show what the corporate-attention-economy looks like when the corporate-attention-economy is being itself.

Laney has been reading the nodal-points too. Laney reads the nodal-points the way I do, but Laney reads the nodal-points through his cough-syrup-damaged cortex, through his attention-economy-substrate, through his ability to see what is about to happen before it happens.

Midpoint Reversal

But the nodal-points are not just attention-events anymore. The nodal-points are also people-events. The nodal-points are also the people who carry the attention-flow. The nodal-points are also the people who are the attention-flow.

The nodal-points are also me. The nodal-points are also Laney. The nodal-points are also the Rooster, who is sick now, who is coughing now, who is lying in the garage-camp's sleeping-corner staring at the ceiling.

The nodal-points are also Chevette, who is bringing food to the Armenians, who is checking on the Lost-Norters, who is the bridge's body the way I am the bridge's eye.

The nodal-points are not just places. The nodal-points are also people. The nodal-points are the places where the bridge's attention-flow becomes a person-flow.

But the nodal-points were not just the bridge's. The nodal-points were also the city's. The city's nodal-points were the places where the city's attention-flow converged, where the corporate-extraction-economy surfaced, where the bridge-community met the tourists, where the Lost-Norters met the Armenians, where the Black Travelers met the Rooster's video-art. The city's nodal-points were the places where the city could be seen. The bridge's nodal-points were the places where the bridge could be seen. The nodal-points were the places where the truth could be made visible.

Climax

I went looking for a new-nodal-point today. I went to the second-tier east, where the bridge-community meets the corporate-extraction-economy. I went to the place where Chevette usually meets the Armenians, where the Black Travelers usually run their saltline, where the Lost-Norters usually set up their temporary-camps.

The nodal-point was not there. The nodal-point had moved.

I asked Chevette where the nodal-point was. Chevette said: "It's moved. It's moved to the third-tier west. It's moved to the place where the Rooster used to film."

I went to the third-tier west. The third-tier west was empty. The third-tier west was not the place where the nodal-point was.

I asked Skinner where the nodal-point was. Skinner said: "The nodal-point is not on the bridge. The nodal-point has gone below. The nodal-point is in the city."

I went below. I went into the city, with my camera, with my film, with my eye for the attention-flow. I found the nodal-point. The nodal-point was a coffee-shop on Folsom Street, where a corporate-extraction-meeting was happening, where the corporate-attention-economy was being itself, where the city's underlying-conditions were surfacing.

Resolution

I made a video. I made a video of the coffee-shop, of the corporate-extraction-meeting, of the city's underlying-conditions surfacing. I made the video that the Rooster would have wanted me to make.

The video was not finished. The video needed editing. The video needed the Rooster's attention-economy-reading.

But the Rooster was sick. But the Rooster was coughing. But the Rooster was lying in the garage-camp's sleeping-corner staring at the ceiling.

The video would have to wait. The video would have to wait for the Rooster to get better. The video would have to wait for the Rooster to come back.

The coffee-shop smelled of burnt-espresso and old-paper. The corporate-extraction-meeting was loud, the kind of loud that the corporate-attention-economy makes when it thinks nobody is watching. I could feel the camera's weight in my hands. I could feel the analog-film's warmth under my fingers. I could taste the espresso I had bought but not drunk. I could hear Chevette's voice on the bridge's radio-frequency, telling me to come back. The nodal-point was here. The truth was here.

"The nodal-points were not just places. The nodal-points were also people. The nodal-points were also attention-events. The nodal-points were also the bridge's way of saying: this is where the city's underlying-conditions surface. The nodal-points were the bridge's truth-telling."

I went back to the bridge. I sat in the garage. The Rooster was still sick. The Rooster was still coughing. The Rooster was still staring at the ceiling.

But the bridge was still the bridge. The bridge was still trying to communicate. The bridge was still the place where the truth could be seen.

That was enough. That was what the nodal-points were for.

The corporate-attention-economy at the coffee-shop included Maya, San Francisco, Skinner, and Chevette's name on the bridge's cable-lights. Maya and San Francisco were the corporate-extraction-meeting's participants. Skinner and Chevette were the bridge-community-references that I held in my head while I made the video.
