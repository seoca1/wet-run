도입 (Setup)

The Kombinat's agent had been waiting for Laney outside Laney's hotel in Shinjuku for three days. The agent had been patient. The agent had been, Laney had determined by the second day, Kombinat's patience. Kombinat's patience was not Laney's patience. Kombinat's patience was a work of engineering.

Laney had stayed inside the hotel. The hotel's room had been, Laney had determined, the only room in Tokyo where the nodal perception could rest. The nodal perception had not rested in three days. The nodal perception had been watching the agent through the hotel's walls. The agent's presence had been a data-pattern Laney's perception could not un-see.

The agent had been, Laney had determined, female. The agent had been, Laney had determined, forty-one or forty-two. The agent had been, Laney had determined, Kombinat's. Kombinat's agents, Laney had determined by the second day, were Kombinat's most uniform data-patterns. Kombinat's agents were Kombinat's hands. Kombinat's hands were Kombinat's eyes. Kombinat's eyes were Kombinat's.

전개 (Rising Action)

On the fourth morning, Laney left the hotel. The leaving was a test. The leaving was, Laney had determined, a test the agent would not pass.

The agent followed. The agent followed at a distance the agent had been, Laney had determined, trained to maintain. The agent's training had been, Laney had determined, Kombinat's training. Kombinat's training was, Laney had determined, the world's most uniform.

Laney walked south. The walking was, Laney had determined, not toward anywhere. The walking was, Laney had determined, toward Tokyo's data-patterns. Tokyo's data-patterns were, Laney had determined, the data-patterns the Kombinat wanted. The Kombinat wanted Tokyo's data-patterns. The Kombinat had wanted, Laney had determined, for years.

The agent followed. The agent had not passed the test.

Laney stopped. Laney stopped in front of a kiosk. The kiosk had been, Laney had determined, a coffee kiosk twenty years earlier. The kiosk had been, Laney had determined, a tea kiosk ten years earlier. The kiosk had been, Laney had determined, a node-art kiosk now. The kiosk's display had been, Laney had determined, the kiosk's display.

The agent stopped. The agent stopped behind Laney. The agent's distance had been, Laney had determined, the distance the agent had been trained to maintain. The agent's training had been, Laney had determined, Kombinat's.

Laney turned.

절정 (Climax)

The agent had been, Laney had determined, female. The agent had been, Laney had determined, exactly forty-one. The agent had been, Laney had determined, exactly Kombinat's. The agent's expression had been, Laney had determined, the expression Kombinat's expressions were. Kombinat's expressions were, Laney had determined, expressions of the kind the Kombinat's employees had been trained to maintain.

"You are Laney," the agent said. The saying was, Laney had determined, not a question.

"I am Laney," Laney said.

"You have been avoiding us," the agent said.

"You have been watching me," Laney said.

"We have been watching," the agent said. "We have been waiting. We would like to talk."

"You would like," Laney said. "I would not."

The agent did not react. The agent's reaction had been, Laney had determined, Kombinat's reaction. Kombinat's reactions were, Laney had determined, the reactions the Kombinat's employees had been trained to maintain.

Laney's nodal perception had been, Laney had determined, watching the agent's reaction. Laney's nodal perception had been, Laney had determined, watching the agent's reaction to Laney. Laney's nodal perception had been, Laney had determined, watching the agent's reaction's reaction to Laney.

The watching had been, Laney had determined, a recursive loop. The recursive loop had been, Laney had determined, the data-pattern the Kombinat had been waiting for.

"We have waited," the agent said, "for the data to organize itself."

"The data organizes itself," Laney said, "when the data is ready."

"We believe," the agent said, "the data is ready."

Laney turned. Laney walked. The walking was, Laney had determined, away from the agent. The walking was, Laney had determined, the data Laney's nodal perception had been waiting to give.

The agent did not follow.

결말 (Resolution)

Laney walked back to the hotel. Laney's hotel room had been, Laney had determined, the same hotel room Laney had left. The hotel room had been, Laney had determined, the data-pattern Laney had been waiting to give. The data-pattern was, Laney had determined, the data-pattern the Kombinat had not been given.

Laney sat. Laney's nodal perception rested. The nodal perception had been, Laney had determined, ready to rest. The nodal perception had been, Laney had determined, ready because Laney had walked.

The walking had been, Laney had determined, the data. The data had been, Laney had determined, the answer. The answer had been, Laney had determined, the data the Kombinat had wanted.

The Kombinat had not been given the answer.

Laney had not given the Kombinat the answer.

Laney had walked.
