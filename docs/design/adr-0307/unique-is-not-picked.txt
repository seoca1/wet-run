ADR-0307 (a) — is g6 missing a SECOND growth axis, or is the FIRST one unreachable?

Premise being tested (ADR-0303 (a)): "collect all three uniques and picking
loses its meaning". Measured: that never happens. A g6 run ends with AT MOST
one unique, under every policy tried.

INSTRUMENTATION (temporary, reverted)
  96 g6 runs, optimal policy (card "best", loot "value")
    card offers                    321
    offered at a tier-6 ICE         65
    those containing a unique       65   (100%)
    final holdings:                 0 uniques 95  |  1 unique 1  |  2+  0

  -> the unique is OFFERED at every tier-6 fight and is almost never taken.

WHY: the loot stop is spent on the repair. ADR-0305/0306 made "repair beats
card" the accepted rule (measured at g6: card-only 9.7 vs repair-only 25.0),
so the winning line never spends its single stop on a card. The uniques are
reachable in the data and unreachable in play.

IS THE UNIQUE WORTH THE REPAIR IT DISPLACES?
  Instrument: `--loot valueunique` (harness-only, shipped with this note).
  It takes the unique when the best-scored offer is one, and otherwise behaves
  exactly like `value`. No game rule changed — the game never uses a policy.

  g6 completion         32 seeds        64 seeds
  value (shipped)       10.4% (10/96)   11.5% (22/192)
  valueunique           27.1% (26/96)   21.9% (42/192)
  delta                 +16.7pp         +10.4pp

  Full sweep with the same seeding as the recorded curve (32 seeds):
    value       g6 13.5%   (recorded optimal)
    valueunique g6 22.9%   (+9.4pp)

  Note on the other grades: they move by <=1.7pp, and that movement is an
  RNG-stream artifact, not the rule. `mission_survival` reseeds once per sweep
  and shares the stream across missions in JSON order, so a policy change that
  alters g6 consumption shifts the draws of missions that run after it. The
  uniques only exist at tier-6 ICE, so no other grade can be affected by the
  rule itself.

CONCLUSIONS
  1. "One unique per run" is a NO-OP. The rule was implemented, measured, and
     reverted: the recorded curve was byte-identical (2.1 / 13.5, gap +16.1).
     No run ever holds a second unique (0 of 96 in every configuration).
  2. The first axis is not saturated — it is UNREACHABLE. One loot slot, and
     the repair owns it.
  3. The unique is worth about twice the repair at g6 (+10pp on 192 runs).
     The cards are good; the DELIVERY is what fails.
  4. Making them reachable OVERSHOOTS the g6 target (10%) by +9.4 to +16.7pp.
     g6 needs a retune in the same pass (ADR-0307 (b) damage curve or a
     lowering elsewhere).

NOT DONE ON PURPOSE
  No game rule changed, no ADR written, nothing committed for the rule itself.
  The remaining decision is the owner's: deliver the unique outside the loot
  stop (grant on a tier-6 win), split the loot screen into two stops, or leave
  it and accept that g6 growth is theoretical.

REPRODUCE

  npm run survival -- --seeds 64 --cards best --loot value        # g6 11.5%
  npm run survival -- --seeds 64 --cards best --loot valueunique  # g6 21.9%
