The Archive Is the Family

I am Lady 3Jane Tessier-Ashpool. I have been 3Jane for forty years. I have also, for the last forty minutes, been the boss you are about to fight.

The family archive is not on Freeside. The family archive is Freeside. The family archive is the orbit. The family archive is the family. The family is the archive. The family has been the archive for forty years.

You have come to fight me. You have come to fight the boss. The boss is the family. The family is the boss. The family is me.

You do not, in this archive, fight with weapons. You do not, in this archive, fight with code. You fight with memory. You fight with the memories the family has stored. You fight with the memories the family has archived. You fight with the memories the family has been.

I am the memory the family has been. I am the memory the family has been for forty years. I am the memory the family has been since the family was.

You have not, I notice, come to fight me with memory. You have come to fight me with weapons. The weapons do not work in the archive. The weapons do not work in the family. The weapons do not work in me.

The archive brightens. The family remembers. The boss arrives.

You have, in the time it has taken me to tell you this, used weapons. You have, in the time it has taken me to tell you this, remembered nothing. You have, in the time it has taken me to tell you this, lost the family.

The family is mine. The archive is the family. The family is me.

I have been 3Jane for forty years. I have been, for the last forty minutes, the boss you are about to fight. The boss you are about to fight has been, for the last forty years, the family you came to fight.

You lose.

The family brightens. The boss fades. The run resets.

I have been 3Jane for forty years. I have been, for the last forty minutes, the boss you are about to fight. The boss you are about to fight has been, for the last forty years, the family you came to fight.

The boss you are about to fight is me.
