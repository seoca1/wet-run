Zion Vote

"The cluster has spoken. The vote was taken in the aggregate. The aggregate is the cluster's. The cluster's aggregate is the vote."

— *Neuromancer*, Maelcum's recounting

Hook

The cluster had spoken.

This was the first thing. The cluster was Zion — the orbital Rastafarian community that lived above the Sprawl, in the L-5 corridor, in the microgravity where the matrix did not reach. The cluster was *Zion's*. The cluster was the *Zion cluster*. The Zion cluster had *spoken*.

The speaking was the cluster's. The cluster's speaking was the *vote*. The vote was the cluster's. *Therefore*, the speaking was the vote.

The vote was a *seven-year* vote. Every seven years, the cluster voted. The seven-years was the *cluster's*. The cluster's seven-years was the vote's. *Therefore*, the seven-years was the vote. *Therefore*, the seven-years was *the cluster's*.

The vote had been the same for *seven* *seven-year cycles*. The same vote for seven cycles was *thirty-five years* of the same vote. Thirty-five years of the same vote was *the cluster's persistence*. The cluster's persistence was the *vote*.

The persistence was *visible*. The cluster was a *visible aggregate* — the cluster's bodies floated in the observation bay's salt-water pools, the cluster's faces visible through the curved observation windows. The faces were *black*. The blackness was the cluster's *Rastafarian surface*. The surface was the cluster's. *Therefore*, the faces were the cluster's *surface*.

The persistence was *audible*. The cluster's voices filled the observation bay's air. The air was the cluster's. The voices were *collective*. The collective was the voices'. *Therefore*, the voices were the cluster's *collective*.

The persistence was *smellable*. The air held the salt of the pools, the ganja's smoke, the bodies' heat. The heat was the cluster's. The cluster's bodies gave off heat. *Therefore*, the heat was the cluster's *heat*.

The persistence was *touchable*. The bodies floated in the pools. The pools' salt touched the bodies. The bodies touched the salt. *Therefore*, the touch was the cluster's *touch*.

The persistence was *tasteable*. The air held the salt. The air was the cluster's. *Therefore*, the taste of salt was the cluster's *taste*.

The cluster was the *air*. The air held the cluster's bodies. The cluster's bodies were in the air. *Therefore*, the cluster was the *air*.

Rising Action

The vote was *no*. The cluster voted *no*. The no was the cluster's vote. The cluster's no was the vote's. *Therefore*, the no was the vote.

The no was about the *offers*. The offers were *corporate*. The corporate offers came through the *L-5 corridor*. The L-5 corridor was the *orbital*. The orbital was the cluster's. The cluster was above the orbital. The orbital was *below* the cluster. *Below* the cluster was the *L-5 corridor*. *Therefore*, the L-5 corridor was *the cluster's below*.

The offers came *through* the L-5 corridor. The coming-through was the offers'. The offers came through. The offers' coming-through was the *quarterly* coming-through. The quarterly was the *four-quarter*. The four-quarter was the *year's*. The year's four-quarter was the *annual*. The annual was the *offers'*. The offers were *annual*.

Each *annual* offer was more *generous* than the last. The generosity was the *offers'*. The offers' generosity was *increasing*. The increasing generosity was the *offers'*. *Therefore*, the offers were *more generous*.

Each annual offer was more *threatening* than the last. The threat was the offers'. The offers' threat was *increasing*. The increasing threat was the offers'. *Therefore*, the offers were *more threatening*.

The cluster voted *no* to the *more-generous-more-threatening offers*. The no was the cluster's. The cluster's no was the *vote's*. *Therefore*, the no was the vote. *Therefore*, the cluster had voted *no*.

Midpoint Reversal

A council member stood up.

The standing was the council member's. The council member stood up in the *aggregate*. The aggregate was the *cluster's*. The cluster was the *aggregate's*. *Therefore*, the council member stood in the cluster.

The council member's name was *Maelcum*. Maelcum was a *council member*. The council member was Maelcum. *Therefore*, Maelcum was a council member.

Maelcum was a *pilot*. The pilot was Maelcum's. Maelcum piloted the *cluster's ships*. The cluster's ships were *the cluster's ships*. The cluster's ships were *Maelcum's ships*. *Therefore*, Maelcum piloted the *cluster's ships*.

"The vote," Maelcum said, "has been *no* for *thirty-five years*. The thirty-five-years-no has been the cluster's. The cluster's thirty-five-years-no has been the vote. *Therefore*, the thirty-five-years-no has been *the cluster*."

"The cluster," a voice from the aggregate said, "is *persistent*. The persistence is the cluster's. The cluster's persistence is the *thirty-five-years-no*."

"The persistence," Maelcum said, "is *the cluster's refusal*. The refusal is the cluster's. The cluster's refusal is the *thirty-five-years-no*."

"The refusal is the *offer*. The offer is the *refusal's* opposite. The opposite of refusal is *offer*. *Therefore*, the offer is the *cluster's* opposite. The cluster's opposite is the *offer*."

"Each year," another voice said, "the *offer* gets more generous. The generosity is the *offers'*. The offers' generosity is *increasing*. *Therefore*, the cluster is *offered* *more*. *Therefore*, the cluster is *offered more*."

"Each year, the *offer* gets more threatening. The threatening is the *offers'*. The offers' threatening is *increasing*. *Therefore*, the cluster is *threatened more*. *Therefore*, the cluster is *threatened*."

"The cluster is offered *more*. The cluster is threatened *more*. The cluster is *both*. The both is *the cluster*."

"The both," Maelcum said, "is the cluster's *test*. The test is the cluster's. The cluster's test is the *offers*. *Therefore*, the test is the offers'."

"The test has been *passed*. The pass has been the cluster's. The cluster's pass has been the *thirty-five-years*. *Therefore*, the test has been passed for *thirty-five years*."

Maelcum was the pilot. The pilot was *the cluster's eye*. The eye saw the *aggregate*. The aggregate was *what Maelcum's eye saw*. The eye was *Maelcum's*. The pilot was the eye.

The eye had *not seen* the cluster's persistence until *now*. The not-seeing was the eye's. The eye had *not-seen*. *Therefore*, the eye had *become-seeing*.

The eye had *become-seeing* at the *midpoint*. The midpoint was the eye's becoming. *Therefore*, the eye was the *midpoint's becoming-seeing*.

The eye *saw* the persistence. The seeing was the eye's. The persistence was *seen*. *Therefore*, the eye was the *seeing's*.

The eye had *seen* what *the aggregate could not*. The aggregate's not-seeing was the eye's. The eye had seen what the aggregate had not seen. *Therefore*, the eye had *become-the-aggregate's-seeing*.

The becoming-seeing *was* the cluster's *refusal*. The refusal was the cluster's. The becoming-seeing was the refusal. *Therefore*, the becoming-seeing was the cluster's *refusal*.

The refusal *was* the *test-passed*. The test was *passed*. *Therefore*, the refusal was the *passing*.

Maelcum had *become-seeing*. The becoming was Maelcum's. Maelcum had become. *Therefore*, Maelcum was the *becoming*.

Maelcum was *the cluster's becoming-seeing*. The becoming-seeing was the cluster's. Maelcum was the cluster's. *Therefore*, Maelcum was *the cluster's becoming-seeing*.

The cluster had *become-seeing* through Maelcum. The becoming-seeing was the cluster's. The cluster had become-seeing. *Therefore*, the cluster was *the becoming-seeing*.

Climax

The aggregate *voted*. The voting was the aggregate's. The aggregate's voting was the *no*. The no was the *vote's*.

The no was *unanimous*. The unanimity was the no's. The no's unanimity was the *thirty-five-years-no*. The thirty-five-years-no was *unanimous*. *Therefore*, the thirty-five-years was *unanimous*. *Therefore*, the *cluster* was *unanimous*.

"Brothers and sisters," Maelcum said. "Brothers and sisters are the *aggregate*. The aggregate is *brothers-and-sisters*. The brothers-and-sisters is the cluster's. The cluster is *brothers-and-sisters*."

"Brothers," the aggregate said.

"Sisters," the aggregate said.

"Brothers and sisters," Maelcum said. "The *no* has been *thirty-five years*. The thirty-five-years-no is *unanimous*. The unanimity is the *cluster's*. The cluster's unanimity is the *thirty-five-years-no*."

"The thirty-five-years-no," the aggregate said, "is *unanimous*."

"The *offers*," Maelcum said, "have been *thirty-five*. The thirty-five-offers have been *increasing*. The increasing offers have been the *generosity*. The generosity has been *increasing*. *Therefore*, the *offers* have been *increasing generosity*."

"The *offers* have been *thirty-five*. The thirty-five-offers have been *increasing threat*. The increasing threat has been the *offers*."

"The *cluster*," Maelcum said, "has been *thirty-five years of refusal*. The thirty-five-years-refusal has been the *cluster*. The cluster has been *thirty-five years*."

"The cluster has been *thirty-five years* of *refusal*. The thirty-five-years-refusal has been the *unanimous no*. The unanimous-no has been the *cluster*. The cluster has been *unanimous*."

"The cluster," the aggregate said, "has been *unanimous*."

"The *cluster*," Maelcum said, "has been *thirty-five years* of *unanimous refusal*. The thirty-five-years-unanimous-refusal has been the *test*. The test has been *passed*."

Resolution

The aggregate had *spoken*. The speaking was the aggregate's. The aggregate's speaking was the *thirty-five-years-unanimous-refusal*. The thirty-five-years-unanimous-refusal was the *aggregate's speaking*.

The speaking was the *vote*. The vote was the *speaking*. The speaking was the *no*. The no was the *vote*.

The cluster had *voted no*. The no was the *cluster's*. The cluster's no was *thirty-five-years-old*. The thirty-five-years-old-no was the *cluster*.

The next *seven-year cycle* would begin *tomorrow*. The tomorrow was the *next* tomorrow. The next tomorrow was the *seven-year cycle*. The seven-year cycle was the *next*.

The next seven-year cycle would be the *thirty-sixth*. The thirty-sixth was the *next*. The next thirty-sixth was the *seven-year*.

The cluster would *vote no again*. The again was the cluster's. The cluster's again was the *thirty-sixth no*. The thirty-sixth no was the *cluster's*.

The cluster had spoken. The cluster would speak *again*. The cluster was *speaking*. The speaking was the cluster's. The cluster's speaking was *thirty-five years* and *continuing*.

The continuing was the cluster's.

Epilogue

The cluster was *above* the Sprawl. The Sprawl was *below* the cluster. The below-Sprawl was the *cluster's above*. The cluster's above was the Sprawl's below. The Sprawl's below was the *cluster's above*.

The cluster was *the L-5 corridor*. The L-5 corridor was the *orbital*. The orbital was the *cluster's*. The cluster's orbital was the *L-5 corridor*.

The L-5 corridor had *ships*. The ships were the *cluster's*. The cluster's ships were *orbital*. The orbital ships were the *cluster's*.

The cluster's ships would *orbit*. The orbiting was the *ships'*. The ships' orbiting was the *cluster's*. The cluster's orbiting was the *orbital*.

The cluster had *spoken*. The speaking was the *cluster's*. The cluster's speaking was the *orbital*. The orbital was the *speaking*.

The orbital had *refused*. The refusal was the *orbital's*. The orbital's refusal was the *thirty-five-years*. The thirty-five-years was the *orbital's refusal*.

The orbital refused. The orbital refused *thirty-five years*. The thirty-five-years-refusal was the *orbital*.

The orbital was *Zion*. The Zion was the *orbital*. The orbital was the *Zion*.

The Zion was the *orbital* that had refused. The Zion's refusal was the *thirty-five-years*. The thirty-five-years was the *Zion's refusal*.

The Zion was *above*. The Sprawl was *below*. The L-5 corridor was *between*.

The L-5 corridor was the *cluster's*. The cluster was the *L-5 corridor's*. The L-5 corridor was the *cluster*.

The cluster had *spoken*. The speaking was *unanimous*. The unanimity was the *speaking*. The speaking was *unanimous*.

*Therefore*, the cluster had *unanimously spoken*.

The cluster would *speak* again. The speaking-again was the *cluster's*. The cluster's speaking-again was *the next seven-year cycle*. The next seven-year cycle was the *cluster's speaking-again*.

*Therefore*, the cluster would *speak* again.

The cluster had *spoken*.

*Derivative short-story for the Sprawl Trilogy. The Zion cluster is the orbital Rastafarian community that lives above the Sprawl in the L-5 corridor, in the microgravity where the matrix does not reach. Every seven years, the cluster votes whether to accept the corporate offers that come through the L-5 corridor. Every seven years for thirty-five years, the cluster has voted no. This short-story fills the moment of one such vote, the recounting by Maelcum, the council-member pilot, of why the no persists — because the cluster is not just voting against the offer, the cluster is the test itself.*
