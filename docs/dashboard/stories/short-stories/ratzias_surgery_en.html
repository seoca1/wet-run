Ratzia's Surgery

A BAMA Surgeon Between Wages and Talent

282 wordsSprawl Trilogy

The clinic had no name on the floor. The clinic had no sign on the building.

Ratzia had worked there for nine years. He had worked on bodies that had been thrown away by other surgeons. He had worked on bodies that other surgeons would not touch.

Tonight the body on the table was a young man from Ninsei. The young man had been working with BAMA wage slaves, the kind who could not afford a real clinic. The young man had been caught stealing a deck. The young man had been punished.

The young man's nervous system was a ruin. The young man's nervous system had been ruined by the people who had punished him.

Ratzia washed his hands. Ratzia set out his tools.

A woman stood at the door. The woman was from Chiba. The woman wore a black suit. The woman's eyes were bright.

"Save him if you can," she said. "If you can't, make sure he can't testify."

Ratzia looked at the body. Ratzia looked at the woman.

"I save what I can," he said. "I don't make what I save unable to testify."

"The job pays more," she said.

"The job pays more and I won't take it."

She shrugged. She left.

Ratzia worked for six hours. The young man would not testify. The young man would not walk normally. The young man would live.

In the morning, Ratzia went home. Ratzia had a small apartment above a noodle shop. Ratzia did not own the apartment. Ratzia had never owned anything.

He drank tea. He read the newspaper. He did not think about the young man. He had done his work.

The clinic had no name. The clinic had no sign.

Tomorrow someone else would come.
