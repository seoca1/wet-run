The Oracle's Cough-Syrup Reading

1084 wordsBridge Trilogy

"I am the Oracle. I am the bridge's bar-reader. I am the kind of person who sits in the bridge's bar and reads what-the-bridge-is-doing."

Hook

My name is Oracle. My name was given-to-me by the bridge-community because I am the kind of person who can-read-what-the-bridge-is-doing. My name was not given-to-me by my-parents. My name was given-to-me by the bridge.

I have been the Oracle for fifteen years. I have been reading the bridge's bar for fifteen years. I have been sitting in the bridge's bar-and-reading what-the-bridge-is-doing for fifteen years.

The bridge's bar is not a real-bar. The bridge's bar is the kind of place where the bridge-community-gathers, the kind of place where the bridge-community-drinks, the kind of place where the bridge-community-sleeps.

I have been reading the bridge's bar for fifteen years. I have been reading the bar's customers. I have been reading the bar's atmosphere. I have been reading the bar's attention-flow.

I have been reading the bar's attention-flow since before attention-flow was a thing. I have been reading the bar's attention-flow since attention-flow was just the kind of thing-that-the-bridge's-collective-mind-noticed.

Rising Action

Laney is the kind of person who reads attention-flow the way I read attention-flow. Laney is the kind of person who has-the-gift, the kind of person who can-see-what-attention-flow-is-doing.

Laney does not know he is the kind of person who has-the-gift. Laney does not know that he is the kind of person who can-see-what-attention-flow-is-doing. Laney does not know that he is the kind of person who can-read-the-bridge's-attention-flow.

Laney knows he can read the bar. Laney knows he can read the bridge. Laney knows he can read the kind of thing that the bridge-community-is-doing.

Laney does not know that he is the kind of person who can read what-the-bridge-is-about-to-do. Laney does not know that he is the kind of person who can read what-the-bridge-will-become.

I am the kind of person who can see-what-Laney-can-see. I am the kind of person who can see-what-Laney-will-become.

Laney will become the kind of person who sees-what-the-bridge-is-about-to-do. Laney will become the kind of person who reads the bridge's nodal-points. Laney will become the kind of person who guides the bridge's collective-mind.

I am the Oracle. I am the bridge's bar-reader. I am the kind of person who has-been-reading-the-bridge-for-fifteen-years.

Midpoint Reversal

But the bridge is changing. The bridge is changing in the kind of way that the bridge's-collective-mind-does-not-yet-notice.

The bridge is changing because Laney is changing. Laney is changing because Laney's-cough-syrup-is-changing Laney. Laney's-cough-syrup is changing Laney because Laney's-cough-syrup-is-damaging-Laney's-cortex.

Laney's-cortex is the kind of cortex that can read the bridge's-attention-flow. Laney's-cortex is the kind of cortex that can read the bar's attention-flow. Laney's-cortex is the kind of cortex that can read the attention-flow-of-everything.

Laney's-cortex is being damaged. Laney's-cortex is being damaged by the cough-syrup. Laney's-cortex is being damaged in the kind of way that the bridge-community-does-not-understand.

But the bridge was changing in the kind of way-that-the-Oracle-could-not-stop. The bridge was changing in the kind of way-that-the-corporate-attention-economy-was-changing-it. The bridge was changing in the kind of way-that-everything-was-changing. But the Oracle could still read. The Oracle could still see-the-bridge's-future. The Oracle could still see-the-kind-of-thing-that-the-bridge-would-become.

The bridge-community thinks Laney's-cough-syrup is just a drug. The bridge-community thinks Laney's-cough-syrup is just the kind of thing-that-a-person-takes-to-dampen-attention-flow.

But the cough-syrup is more-than-a-drug. The cough-syrup is the kind of thing-that-damages-the-cortex. The cough-syrup is the kind of thing-that-makes-Laney-less-able-to-read-attention-flow.

Climax

When Laney comes to the bar, Laney is the kind of person who has-taken-too-much-cough-syrup. When Laney comes to the bar, Laney is the kind of person who-can-no-longer-read-attention-flow.

I am the Oracle. I am the kind of person who can see what Laney can-no-longer-do.

Laney cannot read the bar. Laney cannot read the bridge. Laney cannot read the kind of thing-that-Laney-used-to-read.

The bridge is changing. The bridge is changing in the kind of way that Laney-can-no-longer-read. The bridge is changing in the kind of way that the bridge-community-can-no-longer-read.

But the Oracle can still read. The Oracle can still read the kind of thing that the bridge is doing.

The Oracle can read the bridge's-nodal-points. The Oracle can read the kind of thing-that-the-bridge-is-becoming. The Oracle can read the kind of thing-that-the-bridge-will-become.

The bridge will become the kind of place where attention-flow is the kind of thing-that-everyone-can-see. The bridge will become the kind of place where attention-flow is the kind of thing-that-everyone-can-read.

The Oracle can see this. The Oracle can read the bridge's future. The Oracle can read the kind of thing-that-the-bridge-will-become.

Resolution

I am the Oracle. I am the bridge's bar-reader. I am the kind of person who sits-in-the-bar-and-reads-the-bridge.

Laney is the kind of person who cannot-read-the-bridge-anymore. Laney is the kind of person who has-taken-too-much-cough-syrup. Laney is the kind of person whose-cortex-has-been-damaged.

But the bridge does not need Laney. The bridge does not need Laney because the Oracle can read-the-bridge. The Oracle can read the kind of thing-that-Laney-used-to-read.

The Oracle can see the bridge's future. The Oracle can read the bridge's-attention-flow. The Oracle can see the kind of thing-that-the-bridge-will-become.
